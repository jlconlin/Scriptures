# 1 Kings: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Where Gihon was: the writer's sources disagree (Keil: west of the city; the Cambridge Bible: the Kidron). No Latter-day Saint source settles it beyond Huntsman's 'base of the Mount of Olives'.
- **2:** No Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source says whether David's charge against Joab and Shimei was just; only Keil answers, and the chapter gives his answer for Joab as one older reading.
- **4:** How were Solomon's twelve districts drawn, and did they follow or cut across the tribal boundaries (4:7–19)? The only source, the Student Manual relaying a popular book, conflicts with the tribal names in the verses.
- **6:** What did the cherubim, palm trees and open flowers carved on the walls and doors mean (6:29–35)? No Latter-day Saint source read gives them a meaning; Come, Follow Me only invites readers to ponder them, beside Gen. 3:24.
- **7:** Why does the circumference come to 30 cubits for a diameter of 10 (7:23)? Only Keil answers it, and a third older-commentary note would exceed the limit; Why is the porch of judgment (7:7) described here, and is it tied to the understanding heart of 3:9? No source makes the link; Why was the bronze cast in the clay ground of the Jordan valley between Succoth and Zarthan (7:46)?.
- **9:** Where Hazor, Gezer, Tadmor, Baalath and the Millo were, and what archaeology shows of Solomon's gates: no Latter-day Saint or institutional source was read for them; Why Kings has 420 talents and Chronicles 450 (9:28): no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source was found; the one explanation read was Keil's and was cut to keep commentary under a third of the notes.
- **10:** 10:14: whether the figure 666 means anything (a symbol, or a round estimate): no Latter-day Saint source read answers it.
