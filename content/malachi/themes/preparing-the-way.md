Malachi calls the priest the Lord’s messenger, and promises a messenger to prepare the way, the Lord who comes to His temple, and Elijah before the day. The arc runs in the book’s order and ends at the Kirtland Temple, where the Lord came first and Elijah after.

| Step | Passage | The words |
|---|---|---|
| The priest | [[Mal. 2:6–7]] | “he walked with me… and did turn many away from iniquity… he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts” |

## The priest, a messenger

The Lord says of Levi, “he walked with me in peace and equity, and did turn many away from iniquity,” and of the priest, “the priest’s lips should keep knowledge… for he is the messenger of the LORD of hosts” ([[Mal. 2:6–7]]). “Messenger” here is “teacher, tool, representative.”[@ot-manual-34] The same Hebrew word, *malʾak*, is used twice in 3:1, and the name Malachi means “my messenger.”[@oshb-wlc][@scripture-helps-mal] What the priests did instead is on [From Polluted Offerings to an Offering in Righteousness](/malachi/themes/purified-offering/).

## “My messenger”

“Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me” ([[Mal. 3:1]]). “Prepare the way” uses the same verb and noun as [[Isa. 40:3]] ([The New Exodus and the Highway](/isaiah/themes/new-exodus/#prepare-the-way)).[@oshb-wlc] Jesus says of John, “This is he, of whom it is written, Behold, I send my messenger before thy face, which shall prepare thy way before thee” ([[Matt. 11:10]]); His wording reads “before thy face” where Malachi has “before me.” John fulfilled this, and “in our dispensation, the Prophet Joseph Smith was sent as a messenger to prepare the way for the Savior’s Second Coming.”[@scripture-helps-mal] The Lord told Sidney Rigdon he was “sent forth, even as John, to prepare the way before me” ([[D&C 35:4]]).

## The Lord Himself

“And the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple, even the messenger of the covenant, whom ye delight in” ([[Mal. 3:1]]). “Messenger of the covenant” is “a title of Jesus Christ,” and the prophecy “likely has multiple fulfillments,” among them His appearance in the Kirtland Temple on April 3, 1836, and the Second Coming.[@scripture-helps-mal][@bd-christ-names] Jesus gave these words to the Nephites ([[3 Ne. 24:1]]), and the Lord says, “I am Jesus Christ, the Son of God… I will suddenly come to my temple” ([[D&C 36:8]]). The refiner who follows is on [the offering page](/malachi/themes/purified-offering/), and the answer to the question of 2:17 on [The God of Judgment](/malachi/themes/god-of-judgment/).

## Elijah, before the day

“Behold, I will send you Elijah the prophet before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the LORD” ([[Mal. 4:5]]). Set beside Levi, who “did turn many away from iniquity” ([[Mal. 2:6]]), Elijah “shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children” ([[Mal. 4:6]]): the Hebrew verb is the same, and so is “I will send” in 3:1 and 4:5.[@oshb-wlc]

Moroni gave the verses in other words: “I will reveal unto you the Priesthood, by the hand of Elijah” ([[D&C 2:1]]), and, in place of “smite the earth with a curse,” “the whole earth would be utterly wasted at his coming” ([[D&C 2:3]]). Gabriel said John would go before the Lord “in the spirit and power of Elias, to turn the hearts of the fathers to the children” ([[Luke 1:17]]). “Elias” is also a title for a forerunner: “anciently two Eliases were spoken of, one as a preparer and the other a restorer.”[@bd-elias] John served as an Elias “in partial, but not complete, fulfillment of Malachi’s prophecy.”[@alford-elijah] The note at [Malachi 4:5](/malachi/4/) takes up Elijah, Elias and John.

## The Lord and Elijah at Kirtland

On April 3, 1836, the Lord said, “I have accepted this house,” and “I will manifest myself to my people in mercy in this house” ([[D&C 110:7]]). “Following the appearance of Jesus Christ, Moses, Elias, and Elijah also came.”[@gong-hosanna-hallelujah] Elijah stood before Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery and said that “the time has fully come, which was spoken of by the mouth of Malachi… Therefore, the keys of this dispensation are committed into your hands” ([[D&C 110:13–16]]). Malachi’s promise of Elijah’s return was “fulfilled in our dispensation,” and the sealing power he restored “authorized Joseph Smith—and all succeeding Presidents of the Lord’s Church—to … place a ratifying seal on priesthood ordinances and covenants, and to seal families eternally.”[@scripture-helps-mal] Elijah conferred the keys on Joseph Smith and Oliver Cowdery “in preparation for the coming of the Lord.”[@bd-elijah]
