
## Reported by chapters 1–4 (not yet sorted)

- (1) The name of the Lord and the fear due to it: despised (1:6), great among the nations (1:11), profaned (1:12), dreadful (1:14); the writer reports the same words in 2:2, 2:5, 3:16, 4:2 and 4:5.
- (1) Offerings without blemish and the offering in righteousness: 1:7-8, 1:13-14 with 3:3-4 and 3 Ne. 9:19-20.
- (1) The "Wherein?" exchanges: 1:2, 1:6, 1:7, 2:17, 3:7, 3:8, with 2:14 and 3:13 worded differently. Probably chapter notes only.
- (2) The messenger (mal'ak): the priest as "the messenger of the LORD of hosts" (2:7), with "my messenger" and "the messenger of the covenant" (3:1) and the prophet's name (1:1). Feeds the brief's one likely candidate.
- (2) The people's questions and the Lord's answers: "Where is the God of judgment?" (2:17), answered in 3:1-5, with the complaint returning at 3:13-15.
- (2) Covenant and treachery: berit in 2:4, 5, 8, 10, 14 (and 3:1); bagad only in 2:10-16. Local to this chapter; it belongs in the notes, not a theme page.
- (3) The messenger who prepares the way (3:1, with 2:7 where the same Hebrew word is the priest, and 4:5; Isa. 40:3; Mark 1:2-3).
- (3) "Where is the God of judgment?" and its answers (2:17; 3:1-5; 3:13-18): probably chapter notes, not a page.
- (3) The priests, the sons of Levi, and their offering (1:6-2:9; 3:3-4; D&C 13; 84:31-34; 128:24).
- (3) Covenant curse and blessing (2:2; 3:9-12, with the vocabulary of Deut. 28).
- (3) Fearing the Lord and His name (1:6, 1:11, 1:14; 3:5; 3:16; 4:2): not counted across the book.
- (4) Roots and branches with fathers and children: 4:1 and 4:6, joined by Elder Christofferson's talk (one chapter only, so probably notes and not a page).
- (4) The two sendings: "I will send my messenger" (3:1) and "I will send you Elijah" (4:5), with John the Baptist and Elias (4:5; Isaiah 40 on the site).
- (4) The day of the Lord as fire and as healing: 3:2-3 (refiner's fire), 4:1-2 (oven, Sun of righteousness), 4:5 (great and dreadful).
- (4) The people's complaint and the Lord's answer: 3:13-18 with 4:1-3 (the same Hebrew words in 3:15 and 4:1).
