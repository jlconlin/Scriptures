In Abraham 3 the earth is first something said: “We will go down… we will make an earth whereon these may dwell” ([[Abr. 3:24]]). Chapter 4 opens in nearly the same words, “Let us go down… And they went down” ([[Abr. 4:1]]), and chapter 5 looks back: the Gods “came down and formed” the heavens and the earth “according to all that which they had said” ([[Abr. 5:4–5]]). In the Church’s reading, the one “like unto God” who speaks in 3:24 is Jesus Christ.

## Before the world was

Abraham is shown “the intelligences that were organized before the world was; and among all these there were many of the noble and great ones” ([[Abr. 3:22]]). See the note on [[Abr. 3:23]].

## “We will go down”

“There stood one among them that was like unto God, and he said unto those who were with him: We will go down… and we will take of these materials, and we will make an earth whereon these may dwell; And we will prove them herewith, to see if they will do all things whatsoever the Lord their God shall command them” ([[Abr. 3:24–25]]). In the Church’s reading the speaker is Jesus Christ, the Creator.[@mcconkie-christ-creation-1982][@pgp-manual-abr-3][@lds-scriptures] One Apostle names “those who were with him” as “Michael and a great host of valiant souls”;[@mcconkie-christ-creation-1982] the Church’s study helps say no more than that others may have helped Jesus Christ in the Creation[@scripture-helps-gen-1-2] (see the note on [[Abr. 4:1]]).

## “Whom shall I send?”

“And the Lord said: Whom shall I send? And one answered like unto the Son of Man: Here am I, send me. And another answered and said: Here am I, send me. And the Lord said: I will send the first. And the second was angry, and kept not his first estate” ([[Abr. 3:27–28]]). The Father presented His plan of salvation,[@scripture-helps-gen-3-4] the only plan presented, and the question was who should be sent to carry it out.[@bednar-divine-nature-2026] For the rest, see [The Father’s One Plan and Satan’s Rebellion](/moses/themes/one-plan-and-rebellion/).

## “Let us go down”

“And then the Lord said: Let us go down. And they went down at the beginning, and they, that is the Gods, organized and formed the heavens and the earth” ([[Abr. 4:1]]). Read as one continuous narrative, the council “decides on a course of action in 3:24–28 and then executes that decision beginning here”;[@smoot-pgp-study-edition-abr-4] the heading in the Church’s edition calls chapter 4 the Gods’ plans for the six days and puts the bringing to pass in chapter 5.[@lds-scriptures] The note on [[Abr. 4:18]] takes up how much of chapter 4 is told as planned. Abraham 4 does not say whether “the Lord” of 4:1 is the Father or the Son.[@lds-scriptures] For the days, see [Abraham 4](/abraham/4/), [Genesis 1](/genesis/1/) and [Moses 2](/moses/2/).

## Counsel, again

“The Gods took counsel among themselves and said: Let us go down and form man in our image” ([[Abr. 4:26]]). The book uses “plan” once, in the account of the fifth time: the Gods “saw that they would be obeyed, and that their plan was good” ([[Abr. 4:21]]).[@lds-scriptures] On “obeyed” where Genesis has “good,” see the note on [[Abr. 4:18]].

## “According to all that which they had said”

“On the seventh time we will end our work, which we have counseled” ([[Abr. 5:2]]). “Counseled” stands five times in 5:2–5, and “counsel” in all its forms six times in the book, all in 4:26 and 5:2–5.[@lds-scriptures] Then: “the Gods came down and formed these the generations of the heavens and of the earth… According to all that which they had said” ([[Abr. 5:4–5]]). The counsel of chapter 5 “appears to be a natural continuation” of the council in chapter 3.[@smoot-divine-council] See [Abraham 5](/abraham/5/).

## The same words, said and done

The notes on [[Abr. 4:1]] and [[Abr. 5:4]] set the first and last of these together. The full list, gathered here: “We will go down” ([[Abr. 3:24]]), “Let us go down” ([[Abr. 4:1]], [[Abr. 4:26]]), “they went down” ([[Abr. 4:1]]), “the Gods went down” ([[Abr. 4:27]]), “the Gods came down” ([[Abr. 5:4]]); and “we will make an earth” ([[Abr. 3:24]]), “organized and formed the heavens and the earth” ([[Abr. 4:1]]), “came down and formed” ([[Abr. 5:4]]).

The book’s other “go down” and “come down” are Abraham’s journey ([[Abr. 2:21]]), the Lord’s words to Abraham ([[Abr. 1:16–17]]; [[Abr. 3:21]]) and a right that “came down from the fathers” ([[Abr. 1:3]]).[@lds-scriptures] The whole Creation was planned by God before the work began,[@nelson-creation-2000] and Abraham “gives a blueprint as it were of the Creation,” then “says they performed as they had planned.”[@mcconkie-christ-creation-1982]

## By the Son

Under His Father’s direction Jesus Christ was the creator of the earth,[@living-christ] and God, working through His Son, organized the elements.[@gs-create-creation] The Father says in Moses: “by the Son I created them, which is mine Only Begotten” ([[Moses 1:33]]), and “by mine Only Begotten I created these things” ([[Moses 2:1]]).[@lds-scriptures] John says, “All things were made by him” ([[John 1:3]]). The Creator of [[Abr. 3:24]] is also the one “chosen and sent” in [[Abr. 3:27–28]], in the Church’s reading.[@pgp-manual-abr-3]
