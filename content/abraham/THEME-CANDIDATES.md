- (1) The priesthood of the fathers and how Abraham received it: 1:2-4, 18, 26-27, 31 (with D&C 84:14; 107:40-52).
- (1) The records of the fathers: 1:28, 31 (no note now; see unanswered).
- (1) Jehovah who delivers, and the name Jehovah known to the patriarchs: 1:15-20 (with 2:8 and JST Ex. 6:3).
- (1) The Lord's name put upon His servant and made known through his ministry: 1:18-19 (with 2:6-11).
- (1) Famine as the Lord's instrument: 1:29-30; 2:1, 5, 17, 21.
- (1) Pharaoh's claim to the priesthood and the lineage of Ham: 1:21-27.
- (2) "Hearken to my voice": 1:5, 1:7, 1:15, 2:6, 2:13 (whose voice differs: Abraham's in 1:7, the Lord's elsewhere).
- (2) The priesthood "right" of the fathers: 1:2-4; 2:9-11; with D&C 84:14 and 110:12.
- (2) Jehovah who delivers and then covenants: 1:16; 2:8, 2:13.
- (2) The covenant and the seed, by birth and by adoption: 2:9-11.
- (2) Famine as the Lord's instrument: 1:29-30; 2:1, 5, 17, 21.
- (3) Greatness as nearness to God (3:2-3, 9, 16, 19, 21).
- (3) The divine council and the Gods (3:22-28; 4:1, 26; 5:2-3).
- (3) Foreordination and the first and second estates (3:22-28).
- (3) Jehovah who delivers (3:20-21 with 1:15-16).
- (3) The Urim and Thummim and the records of the fathers (3:1, 4).
- (3) The facsimiles and the text (3:3 with Facsimile 2, figure 1; 1:12-14 with Facsimile 1).
- (4) Obedience of the elements beside the proving of the spirits: Abr. 2:7; 3:25; 4:10, 12, 18, 21, 25, 31. Cut from the chapter as its own connection, which a theme page may make.
- (4) The Gods in council, "Let us go down": Abr. 3:22–24; 4:1, 26–27; 5:2–5.
- (4) Planning and bringing to pass ("counseled", "their plan was good", "according to all that which they had said"): Abr. 3:24–25; 4:21, 31; 5:1–5.
- (4) The facsimile explanations and the text ("expanse", Raukeeyang): Abr. 4:6 with Facsimile 1, figure 12, and Facsimile 2, figure 4; also 1:12–14 and chapter 3.
- (5) The plan carried out: Abr. 3:24-25; 4:1; 5:2-5 ("counseled" five times, "came down", "according to all that which they had said").
- (5) Kolob and the reckoning of time: Abr. 3:4; 5:13; Facsimile 2, figure 1.
