# Deuteronomy: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.
- **4:** 4:26 'I call heaven and earth to witness': why call heaven and earth as witnesses? No note.
- **5:** 5:28: no Latter-day Saint source found that explains why the LORD approves the people's request while D&C 84 says they hardened their hearts; 5:3: no Latter-day Saint source found on who 'our fathers' are, or on how a covenant made at Horeb binds the Moab generation.
- **8:** 8:9: what 'brass' dug from the hills means (copper ore). The site has no sourced note on it, so the plain wording keeps the King James word.
- **9:** 9:22–23: why Moses lists Taberah, Massah and Kibroth-hattaavah out of journey order. Only Keil answers it, and the brief's limit of a third of the notes kept it out; 9:22: where Taberah and Kibroth-hattaavah were. Only the 1980 Student Manual says, the Bible Dictionary has no entries for them, and the note had nothing beyond the manual to stand with it.
- **10:** Whether Mosera (Deut. 10:6) and Moseroth (Num. 33:30–31) are the same place, and why Numbers puts Aaron's death on mount Hor: no source read settles either.
- **11:** 11:21 'as the days of heaven upon the earth': no source beyond footnote 21b's alternative reading was used (the writer left it to the footnote); 11:29: whether an altar has been found on Mount Ebal; the writer found no Latter-day Saint source.
- **12:** What does it mean for the LORD to 'cause his name to dwell' in a place (12:5, 11)? The only source read was Keil, and the note built on it was cut. A Latter-day Saint source on the name in the temple would let a note return.
- **14:** Why the clean-animal list names seven wild game animals (14:5) and how they are identified: the only source read was Cambridge, so the note was cut.
