# Judges: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **3:** What the pĕsîlîm 'quarries' by Gilgal (3:19, 26), elsewhere 'idols', signify in the story: no source read gives a meaning (from the writer's report).
- **4:** 4:11: Is Heber's Kedesh, near Zaanaim, the same place as Barak's Kedesh-naphtali? The writer found no source that says so; 4:21: Was Jael right to kill a guest? No Latter-day Saint source read gives a moral verdict; only Keil does.
- **5:** 5:4: why the song has the LORD come from Seir and Edom, south and east of Canaan. Only the older commentaries answer it, and the chapter is already at its limit of outside-commentary notes; 5:14: what "a root of them against Amalek" means in Ephraim. No Church footnote and no Latter-day Saint source explains it.
- **7:** What did the drinking test at the water measure (7:5–6)? The Church's sources and Scripture Central say only that its purpose "is not clear.".
- **9:** 9:45: why Abimelech sowed the city with salt. Not written, because the Church's footnote covers it.
- **12:** Why is there a 'mount of the Amalekites' in Ephraim (12:15)? Only the Pulpit Commentary's guess answers it, so the note was cut; Why is Elon's Aijalon placed in Zebulun (12:12)?.
- **13:** Why does Israel not cry to the LORD in 13:1, as in the earlier cycles?; Why does the angel come back to the woman, not to Manoah, after Manoah's prayer?; Why is Manoah's wife left unnamed?.
- **14:** Whether touching the lion's carcass (14:8–9) or the wedding feast (14:10) broke Samson's Nazarite vow: the writer found no source that makes the link; Where Ashkelon lay, in a Church or BYU source (the Pulpit's note on its location was cut to keep the Bible Hub limit).
- **17:** What became of the other nine hundred shekels (verse 4): the text does not say, and no source read answers it.
- **18:** How did the spies recognize the Levite's voice (18:3)? Keil's 'dialect' explanation was cut by the writer as a curiosity, and no Latter-day Saint source answers it; Was Dan right to destroy Laish? No Latter-day Saint source addresses the Danite conquest itself; the note points to the Church's general reading at Josh. 6:21.
- **19:** Why no one in Gibeah took the travellers in (19:15): no source gives a reason beyond the text.
- **20:** Why does the text mention Benjamin's seven hundred left-handed slingers (20:16)? No Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source read says; Why do Benjamin's numbers not agree (26,700 mustered; 25,100 slain in 20:35 but 25,000 in 20:46; 600 left)? The Cambridge Bible only notes that they don't.
- **21:** Does 'the LORD had made a breach in the tribes' (21:15) put the blame on God, when the breach was Israel's own war? No source read answers it; Whether Jephthah's vow (11:30–39) and the oaths of 21 belong together: no source read joins them.
