# Exodus: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 1:15 — were the midwives Hebrew women, or Egyptian midwives to the Hebrews? Only Keil speaks to it (Hebrew); the Cambridge Bible page would not load for the writer; 1:22 — how does Pharaoh's decree stand behind the death of the firstborn (12:29)? No source read joins them; left for chapter 12; 1:5 — how is the seventy counted? Not answered here; the setting points to Genesis 46.
- **2:** Who was Pharaoh's daughter, and why would she defy her father's decree? (2:5-10); Hebrews 11:27 says Moses left Egypt 'not fearing the wrath of the king', but Exodus 2:14-15 says 'Moses feared' and fled; no source read reconciles them (left as an explore bullet); What did Moses's mother see in a 'goodly child' (2:2)? Only the New Testament's 'by faith' and one older commentary speak to it; the note was cut and the question stays with the explore bullet on Hebrews 11.
- **3:** 3:8 "a land flowing with milk and honey": what the phrase meant (the writer read nothing beyond the plain sense); 3:5: what makes the ground holy (only the older commentary says, and it is at its limit); 3:16 "I have surely visited you" beside Joseph's "God will surely visit you" (Gen. 50:24-25): only the older commentary joins them; 3:12: what the token is; the one study read says it is unclear and lists proposals, and the note reports that without choosing; 3:19 "no, not by a mighty hand": the Church footnote ("OR except by power") and the older commentary ("not even") disagree; the paraphrase keeps the King James words and no note treats it.
- **4:** 4:18: why Moses tells Jethro only that he wants to see whether his brethren are alive, and says nothing of the call; 4:25-26: what Zipporah means by 'a bloody husband'; the note says neither the King James text nor the JST explains it; 4:10: how 'I am not eloquent' squares with Stephen's 'mighty in words' (Acts 7:22); no source read joins the two; 4:14: who Aaron is and where he has been; only a clause in setting (three years the elder, 7:7), with the brief placing Aaron at 28:1.
- **5:** 5:1 — Where are the elders, whom the Lord said would go in to Pharaoh with Moses (3:18)? No source read addresses it; 5:5 — What does Pharaoh mean by 'the people of the land now are many'? Only the older commentary treats it; 5:7 — What does chopped straw do in a mud brick? Only the Cambridge Bible says; not cited; 5:16 — Why do translations differ on whose fault it is? The note lists the renderings; no readable source explains the Hebrew grammar behind them.
- **6:** 6:7: where else 'I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God' is said, and what the formula means; no source read treats it at this verse; 6:26: why a people in bondage are called 'armies'; the writer read the Old Testament Minute and BDB but wrote no note, to stay under the Language-note limit; 6:9: what 'anguish of spirit' is in the Hebrew; the thread gives only the verse's own reason and one older reading; 6:23: Elisheba's tribe (Judah, through Naashon), and what the marriage joins; only Keil identifies it.
- **7:** 7:22: if all the water was already blood, what water did the magicians turn? (Verse 24's digging is only paraphrased; no source read answers it.); 7:25: does the “seven days” signify anything?.
- **8:** 8:10: why Pharaoh says 'To morrow' and not at once; no source read gives a reason; 8:16: why the third plague comes with no warning to Pharaoh; no Latter-day Saint source read treats the plagues' arrangement, and the note only observes it; 8:19: what the magicians meant by 'the finger of God'; no Church source says, and only one older reading is given.
- **9:** 9:14: what does 'send all my plagues upon thine heart' mean? Only the older commentary treats it, and the chapter's allowance for that commentary is used; Why does the sixth plague come with no warning, and is there a pattern in the ten? No Latter-day Saint source read sets it out; chapter 7 owns the plagues as a whole; Rom. 9:17: how do Latter-day Saint sources explain Paul's use of verse 16? None was found by the writer; the chapter gives a sentence and points to Ex. 4:21.
- **10:** 10:29 with 12:31: Moses says he will see Pharaoh's face no more, yet Pharaoh sends for him on the Passover night. The note covers 11:4-8, but no source read addresses 12:31; 10:11: why Pharaoh would let only the men go. Only Keil and the Cambridge Bible answer it, and the writer left it out to stay within the limit on those two; 10:16-17: whether Pharaoh's confession is sincere. No source read addresses it; 10:7: why Pharaoh's servants now side against him. No source read addresses it.
- **11:** 11:7: whom 'that ye may know' addresses (the 'ye' is plural, in a speech to Pharaoh): Egypt's court or Israel? Only the older commentary says Israel; no Church, BYU or Scripture Central source read addresses it; 11:3: why Moses was 'very great' in Egypt. Answered only from the verse itself; no Latter-day Saint source read says more; 11:5: did every firstborn die, and how is that to be understood? Deferred to the note at Ex. 12:29 as the brief directs. The writer reports that the Church's appendix on violence has nothing on the tenth plague.
- **12:** Why the lamb is roasted and not boiled or eaten raw (12:8-9); Why the lamb is kept four days, from the tenth to the fourteenth (12:3, 6); What being 'cut off from Israel' meant in practice (12:15, 19); Who the mixed multitude were and why they left with Israel (12:38); How the LORD and 'the destroyer' are related (12:23): the note says the chapter does not explain it.
- **13:** Why is the instruction to tell a son given twice (verses 8 and 14), once unasked and once in answer to his question? No source read said; Why is the unredeemed donkey's neck broken rather than the animal slaughtered? Only the Cambridge commentary gives a reason, and it was not carried over.
