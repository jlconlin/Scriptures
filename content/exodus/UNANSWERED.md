# Exodus: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 1:15 — were the midwives Hebrew women, or Egyptian midwives to the Hebrews? Only Keil speaks to it (Hebrew); the Cambridge Bible page would not load for the writer; 1:22 — how does Pharaoh's decree stand behind the death of the firstborn (12:29)? No source read joins them; left for chapter 12; 1:5 — how is the seventy counted? Not answered here; the setting points to Genesis 46.
- **2:** Who was Pharaoh's daughter, and why would she defy her father's decree? (2:5-10); Hebrews 11:27 says Moses left Egypt 'not fearing the wrath of the king', but Exodus 2:14-15 says 'Moses feared' and fled; no source read reconciles them (left as an explore bullet); What did Moses's mother see in a 'goodly child' (2:2)? Only the New Testament's 'by faith' and one older commentary speak to it; the note was cut and the question stays with the explore bullet on Hebrews 11.
- **3:** 3:8 "a land flowing with milk and honey": what the phrase meant (the writer read nothing beyond the plain sense); 3:5: what makes the ground holy (only the older commentary says, and it is at its limit); 3:16 "I have surely visited you" beside Joseph's "God will surely visit you" (Gen. 50:24-25): only the older commentary joins them; 3:12: what the token is; the one study read says it is unclear and lists proposals, and the note reports that without choosing; 3:19 "no, not by a mighty hand": the Church footnote ("OR except by power") and the older commentary ("not even") disagree; the paraphrase keeps the King James words and no note treats it.
- **4:** 4:18: why Moses tells Jethro only that he wants to see whether his brethren are alive, and says nothing of the call; 4:25-26: what Zipporah means by 'a bloody husband'; the note says neither the King James text nor the JST explains it; 4:10: how 'I am not eloquent' squares with Stephen's 'mighty in words' (Acts 7:22); no source read joins the two; 4:14: who Aaron is and where he has been; only a clause in setting (three years the elder, 7:7), with the brief placing Aaron at 28:1.
- **5:** 5:1 — Where are the elders, whom the Lord said would go in to Pharaoh with Moses (3:18)? No source read addresses it; 5:5 — What does Pharaoh mean by 'the people of the land now are many'? Only the older commentary treats it; 5:7 — What does chopped straw do in a mud brick? Only the Cambridge Bible says; not cited; 5:16 — Why do translations differ on whose fault it is? The note lists the renderings; no readable source explains the Hebrew grammar behind them.
- **6:** 6:7: where else 'I will take you to me for a people, and I will be to you a God' is said, and what the formula means; no source read treats it at this verse; 6:26: why a people in bondage are called 'armies'; the writer read the Old Testament Minute and BDB but wrote no note, to stay under the Language-note limit; 6:9: what 'anguish of spirit' is in the Hebrew; the thread gives only the verse's own reason and one older reading; 6:23: Elisheba's tribe (Judah, through Naashon), and what the marriage joins; only Keil identifies it.
- **7:** 7:22: if all the water was already blood, what water did the magicians turn? (Verse 24's digging is only paraphrased; no source read answers it.); 7:25: does the “seven days” signify anything?.
- **8:** 8:10: why Pharaoh says 'To morrow' and not at once; no source read gives a reason; 8:16: why the third plague comes with no warning to Pharaoh; no Latter-day Saint source read treats the plagues' arrangement, and the note only observes it; 8:19: what the magicians meant by 'the finger of God'; no Church source says, and only one older reading is given.
- **9:** 9:14: what does 'send all my plagues upon thine heart' mean? Only the older commentary treats it, and the chapter's allowance for that commentary is used; Why does the sixth plague come with no warning, and is there a pattern in the ten? No Latter-day Saint source read sets it out; chapter 7 owns the plagues as a whole; Rom. 9:17: how do Latter-day Saint sources explain Paul's use of verse 16? None was found by the writer; the chapter gives a sentence and points to Ex. 4:21.
- **10:** 10:29 with 12:31: Moses says he will see Pharaoh's face no more, yet Pharaoh sends for him on the Passover night. The note covers 11:4-8, but no source read addresses 12:31; 10:11: why Pharaoh would let only the men go. Only Keil and the Cambridge Bible answer it, and the writer left it out to stay within the limit on those two; 10:16-17: whether Pharaoh's confession is sincere. No source read addresses it; 10:7: why Pharaoh's servants now side against him. No source read addresses it.
- **11:** 11:7: whom 'that ye may know' addresses (the 'ye' is plural, in a speech to Pharaoh): Egypt's court or Israel? Only the older commentary says Israel; no Church, BYU or Scripture Central source read addresses it; 11:3: why Moses was 'very great' in Egypt. Answered only from the verse itself; no Latter-day Saint source read says more; 11:5: did every firstborn die, and how is that to be understood? Deferred to the note at Ex. 12:29 as the brief directs. The writer reports that the Church's appendix on violence has nothing on the tenth plague.
- **12:** Why the lamb is roasted and not boiled or eaten raw (12:8-9); Why the lamb is kept four days, from the tenth to the fourteenth (12:3, 6); What being 'cut off from Israel' meant in practice (12:15, 19); Who the mixed multitude were and why they left with Israel (12:38); How the LORD and 'the destroyer' are related (12:23): the note says the chapter does not explain it.
- **13:** Why is the instruction to tell a son given twice (verses 8 and 14), once unasked and once in answer to his question? No source read said; Why is the unredeemed donkey's neck broken rather than the animal slaughtered? Only the Cambridge commentary gives a reason, and it was not carried over.
- **14:** 14:7: what the 'six hundred chosen chariots' and 'captains over every one of them' were. No Latter-day Saint source treats it; the writer read only the lexicon and the Cambridge commentary and cut it; 14:28: whether Pharaoh himself died in the sea. Nothing read addresses it; note 14:27 gives only the wording of Ps. 136:15; 14:22: what Paul means by 'baptized unto Moses', and his warning in 1 Cor. 10:5-11. Not read in a source.
- **15:** 15:12: why "the earth swallowed them" when the army drowned (only the older commentary answers, by a contested reading of the tenses); 15:20: why Miriam is called "the sister of Aaron" and not of Moses (only the older commentary); 15:25: what the tree at Marah was (the text does not say; no source read); 15:27: whether the twelve wells and seventy palms signify anything (no Latter-day Saint source read; the older commentary doubts the proposal it reports).
- **16:** 16:10: what the people saw when "the glory of the LORD appeared in the cloud", and what is this verse's own beside 13:21 and 24:16. The writer found no source; the thread only points to those two verses; 16:13: whether the quails here are the same event as Numbers 11, and why the meat gets one verse while the bread gets the chapter. No source read distinguishes the accounts; there is an explore bullet only.
- **17:** 17:6 / 1 Cor. 10:4: what Paul means by the Rock 'that followed them'. No source read explains it; 17:10, 12: whether this Hur is the grandfather of Bezaleel (31:2). The Bible Dictionary gives only this chapter and 24:14.
- **18:** 18:12: why Jethro, and not Aaron or Moses, offers the sacrifice. No Latter-day Saint source the writer read says more than that he was a high priest; 18:27 with Num. 10:29–32: whether Hobab is Jethro and whether this is the same visit. Only the Cambridge Bible treats it, by dividing the text among sources; 18:13–26: no teaching of a latter-day prophet or apostle on Jethro's counsel was found (the writer's two searches of the Church's site).
- **19:** Does "the third day" (19:11, 16) signify anything? No source read made anything of it; Why is Moses sent down a second time when he says the bounds are already set (19:23)? The only study read treats the verse as a later insertion, which was left out; Who were "the priests" of 19:22 before Aaron's calling? No Church or Latter-day Saint source was found; the note gives one older reading only.
- **20:** 20:3 "before me": does it leave room for other gods in second place? The lexicon only says "perhaps" for "in preference to," and no Latter-day Saint source read treats the preposition; 20:24 "in all places where I record my name": only the older commentary explains it; 20:7 has no note: Scripture Helps answers what taking the name in vain means beside the verse, and nothing read went further; 20:26: why steps would uncover nakedness, and whether this stands behind the priests' linen breeches (28:42): no source read makes the link.
- **21:** 21:15, 17: was the death penalty for striking or cursing a parent ever carried out? No source read; 21:24: was 'eye for eye' applied literally in Israel or met by payment, and did it limit vengeance? The brief asks 'what it limited', but no source read says so; the note gives only the measure of the penalty; 21:22: does the Hebrew ('her children come out') mean miscarriage or premature birth, and whose harm is the 'mischief'? Only Keil argues it; the Church footnote's reading is followed and the dispute left out; 21:4: why do the wife the master gave, and her children, stay with him? Only one older reading (Keil) answers.
- **22:** Why is the offense of verse 19 listed here among the capital offenses? No source read went beyond the Student Manual's one sentence; What did 'an oath of the LORD' (verse 11) involve? Only the Church's cross-references were found.
- **23:** Verse 13: why Israel was not even to speak the names of other gods (the thread gives the verse's place; explore points to the notes at Hosea 2:17 and Ps. 16:4); Verse 21: what 'he will not pardon your transgressions' means of the Angel, and why the Church's footnote points to 'Holy Ghost, Loss of.'; Verse 31: what 'the sea of the Philistines' is, and whether these borders were ever reached (explore points to the note at Ps. 72:8); Verse 19: whether any ancient evidence exists for a milk rite; nothing readable was found.
- **24:** Why seventy elders: no readable source says what the number signifies (24:1, 9); Why the cloud covers the mountain six days before the call on the seventh, beyond one study's Sabbath-pattern reading (24:16); Whether Joshua entered the cloud with Moses or waited partway up (24:13, 18; 32:17).
- **25:** How can images of cherubim be commanded (25:18) just after graven images are forbidden (20:4)? No source read for this chapter addresses it; Where did former slaves get the gold, silver and cloth (25:3-7)? The setting gives Ex. 12:35 without a source that ties the two.
- **26:** How the two corner boards of verses 23-24 were made and how the five bars on each wall were arranged: the note on verse 15 says the text is unclear and the question is debated; no source read settles it; Whether the boards were solid planks or open frames: reported as debated (Old Testament Minute), not settled.
- **27:** Why the gate of the court faces east: no source read gives a reason.
- **28:** How the Urim and Thummim worked (28:30): no source claims to know; Why silence of the bells would be fatal (28:35): the text gives no reason, and the sources offer only readings.
- **29:** 29:37: what 'whatsoever toucheth the altar shall be holy' means, and whether Matt. 23:19 bears on it; 29:33: who the 'stranger' is who may not eat of the consecration meal; 29:36: why an altar needs atonement made for it; the note points to 25:17 for the word only.
- **30:** Why the altar of incense is commanded here and not with the table and candlestick in chapter 25: no readable Latter-day Saint source gives a reason; What "cut off from his people" (verses 33, 38) means: not answered here, and chapter 12, where the phrase first occurs, has no note on it either; How much a hin and five hundred shekels of spice were: the writer found no usable Bible Dictionary entry for the hin.
- **31:** 31:17: does 'rested, and was refreshed' mean God tires? The note gives the Hebrew verb and its two other uses and says the passage does not explain the expression; no source read answers the question; 31:14: what 'cut off from among his people' means beside 'put to death'; not researched, and no note on the site to point to.
- **32:** 32:2-3: why earrings in particular (the one study's footnote offers only a guess); 32:34: what 'the day when I visit' refers to (only the older commentary answers, pointing to Num. 14); 32:35: what the plague was; 32:7: why the LORD says 'thy people' to Moses (the thread gives the exchange as the text has it; no Latter-day Saint source read explains it).
- **33:** 33:11: why does Joshua stay in the tent when Moses returns to the camp?; 33:12, 17: what does 'I know thee by name' refer to?.
- **34:** 34:29: why Moses "wist not" that his face shone; 34:6: why the name opens with a doubled "The LORD, The LORD" (the one study read calls it uncertain); 34:34-35: where Moses "went in before the LORD" (the tabernacle is not yet built); 34:6: whether John 1:14 ("full of grace and truth") deliberately echoes the proclamation; no Latter-day Saint source found, so it is only an explore pointer.
- **35:** 35:27: who 'the rulers' were and why their gift is named apart; nothing read goes beyond the footnote; 35:22: what 'offered an offering of gold' (a wave offering in the Hebrew) meant; only the older commentaries explain it, and they disagree, so it was left out.
- **36:** Why the dwelling is made before the furniture it will hold (36:8-38 before 37): the Cambridge volume states the order but no source read gives a reason; Whether a latter-day prophet or apostle has taught from the surplus offering of 36:5-7: the writer's one search found only manuals.
- **37:** Deut. 10:3 has Moses say 'I made an ark of shittim wood' while Ex. 37:1 names Bezaleel: no source read says who is meant; the one commentary found addresses only the order of events; Why the instructions hold the altar of incense back until chapter 30 when the making sets it beside the table and candlestick: only a source-critical answer was found, and it was not carried (the question belongs to Ex. 30:1).
- **38:** 38:8: What was the service of the women at the door, and how could they serve at the door of a tent not yet set up? Only Keil answers (women devoted to fasting and prayer; the description looks forward from the tabernacle's setting up), and it was dropped to hold the commentary limit; 38:21-31: Why was a written account of the metals kept? No source read says; 38:25: Why is the freewill silver of 35:24 not in the account? Keil and the Cambridge commentary only guess.
- **39:** 39:43: what Moses's blessing said. The text does not say and no source read claims to know; the note says so and gives one older reading of what prompted it; 39:1-31: whether the sevenfold count of 'as the LORD commanded Moses' signifies anything. No source read says so of this chapter, and the note draws nothing from the number.
- **40:** 40:15: what 'an everlasting priesthood throughout their generations' means for Aaron's sons. The chapter has no note; the thread points to 28:1, which owns Aaron's priesthood.
