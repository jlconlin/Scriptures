Exodus begins with a king who would kill Israel’s sons and ends this thread with a law that every firstborn son of Israel belongs to the Lord. Between them the Lord calls Israel His firstborn, and the last plague falls on Egypt’s. The thread then leads to Jesus Christ, the Firstborn. Where this page sets one passage beside another, the comparison is the page’s own.

## One firstborn against another

Pharaoh “charged all his people, saying, Every son that is born ye shall cast into the river” ([[Ex. 1:22]]). On Moses’s return to Egypt, the Lord’s message to Pharaoh sets son against son: “Let my son go, that he may serve me: and if thou refuse to let him go, behold, I will slay thy son, even thy firstborn” ([[Ex. 4:23]]). Exodus 4:22–23 gives the reason for the tenth plague.[@lds-scriptures] See the note at [Exodus 4:22](/exodus/4/) for “firstborn” as a word of legal weight.

## The night the firstborn died

Moses announces it: “all the firstborn in the land of Egypt shall die, from the firstborn of Pharaoh that sitteth upon his throne, even unto the firstborn of the maidservant that is behind the mill” ([[Ex. 11:5]]). The blood on the houses is a token: “when I see the blood, I will pass over you” ([[Ex. 12:13]]). Then “at midnight the LORD smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt” ([[Ex. 12:29]]). The passing over “represents Jesus Christ ultimately overcoming death.”[@scripture-helps-ex-7] The death of Egypt’s firstborn is what freed Israel; “in like manner the atoning sacrifice of the Firstborn Son of God freed the children of God from death.”[@ot-manual-ex-11-19] The notes at [Exodus 11:5](/exodus/11/) and [Exodus 12:29](/exodus/12/) take up the rest.

## “It is mine”

The claim on Israel’s firstborn follows from the night: “Sanctify unto me all the firstborn… it is mine” ([[Ex. 13:2]]). The commandment “was related to His sparing of Israel’s firstborn children and animals from the final plague. Having saved the firstborn of the faithful from death, the Lord claimed them as belonging to Him.”[@scripture-helps-ex-7] A father is to tell his son why: the Lord “slew all the firstborn in the land of Egypt… but all the firstborn of my children I redeem” ([[Ex. 13:15]]). For the five shekels and the counting, see [Exodus 13:2](/exodus/13/).

## The Levites in their place

Later the Lord says, “I have taken the Levites from among the children of Israel instead of all the firstborn… Because all the firstborn are mine; for on the day that I smote all the firstborn in the land of Egypt I hallowed unto me all the firstborn in Israel” ([[Num. 3:12–13]]). The Lord “accepted the tribe of Levi in lieu of the first-born of Israel,” and the firstborn beyond their number were redeemed with money.[@ot-manual-ex-11-19] The note at [Exodus 13:2](/exodus/13/) gives the rest.

## The Firstborn

Mary and Joseph “brought him to Jerusalem, to present him to the Lord; (As it is written in the law of the Lord, Every male that openeth the womb shall be called holy to the Lord)” ([[Luke 2:22–23]]). That law is [[Ex. 13:2]]. The ransoming of a firstborn son was “a symbolic reliving of the Passover.”[@skinner-life-jesus] Set that presentation beside these words: “No more would men be required to offer the firstborn lamb from their flock, because the Firstborn of God had come to offer himself as an ‘infinite and eternal sacrifice.’”[@hunter-passover]

“Israel is my son” ([[Ex. 4:22]]) leads to Hosea 11:1,[@lds-scriptures] where Israel, “as a child, was called out of Egypt in similitude of our Lord,”[@lds-scriptures] and Matthew applies “Out of Egypt have I called my son” to Jesus ([[Matt. 2:15]]).

The scriptures say who the Firstborn is. Christ is “the firstborn of every creature” and “the firstborn from the dead” ([[Col. 1:15, 18]]); He says, “I was in the beginning with the Father, and am the Firstborn” ([[D&C 93:21]]). Jesus is the firstborn of the spirit children of our Heavenly Father and the first to rise from the dead.[@bd-firstborn] The commandment to dedicate firstborn sons “can remind us of Jesus Christ, who was the firstborn of all the spirit children of Heavenly Father.”[@scripture-helps-ex-7]
