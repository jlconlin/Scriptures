Exodus moves the Lord’s presence from a mountain into the camp. He promises Moses at the bush that He will be with him, goes before Israel in the cloud, rests on Sinai, and then commands a sanctuary “that I may dwell among them” ([[Ex. 25:8]]). The Hebrew word for that tent, the tabernacle, is a noun made from the verb “dwell.”[@strongs-concordance] After the golden calf the Lord says He will not go up in Israel’s midst; Moses pleads, the Lord relents, and the book ends with His glory filling the tent.

The New Testament carries the thread to Christ. John’s word for “dwelt” in “the Word was made flesh, and dwelt among us” means “to tent or encamp,” used of God residing in the tabernacle of old.[@strongs-greek] The Church’s Topical Guide lists Exodus 13:21 and 29:45 under “God, Presence of” with John 1:14 and Revelation 21:3.[@tg-god-presence-of]

## The path

1. [[Ex. 3:12]]: the promise at the bush, “Certainly I will be with thee.”
2. [[Ex. 13:21]]: the LORD leads the way out, “in a pillar of a cloud” by day and of fire by night.
3. [[Ex. 24:16]]: on the mountain, “the glory of the LORD abode upon mount Sinai.”
4. [[Ex. 25:8]]: the command, “let them make me a sanctuary; that I may dwell among them.”
5. [[Ex. 29:45–46]]: the purpose of the Exodus itself, “that I may dwell among them.”
6. [[Ex. 33:3]]: after the calf, the presence withheld, “I will not go up in the midst of thee.”
7. [[Ex. 33:14–17]]: Moses will not go without Him, and the Lord grants it, “My presence shall go with thee.”
8. [[Ex. 34:9]]: Moses asks again, “let my Lord, I pray thee, go among us.”
9. [[Ex. 40:34–35]]: the tent finished, “the glory of the LORD filled the tabernacle.”
10. [[John 1:14]]: the Word “was made flesh, and dwelt among us.”
11. [[Rev. 21:3]]: at the last, “the tabernacle of God is with men, and he will dwell with them.”
