# Ecclesiastes: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Why 'vanity of vanities': the superlative was read only in Delitzsch, and the writer left it out; Why 'no remembrance' (1:11): nothing beyond the plain words.
- **3:** 3:5: no Latter-day Saint or lexical source explains casting and gathering stones; only Delitzsch does.
- **4:** 4:14: who the old king and the poor youth from prison were; no Latter-day Saint source identifies them, and the older commentary rejects the proposals it examines.
- **5:** 5:8–9: no Church, BYU or Scripture Central source explains who the officials over officials are, or what verse 9 means.
- **6:** 6:2: Why does God give a man riches and not the power to enjoy them? No source I read answers this from the verse itself.
- **7:** 7:16–18: no Latter-day Saint source comments on "be not righteous over much" (as the brief already records); 7:26–28: no Church or Latter-day Saint source explains the Preacher's sayings about the woman and the "one man among a thousand".
- **9:** 9:8: what oil on the head meant (a feast, hospitality) — no source was read for it, so the note leaves it out.
