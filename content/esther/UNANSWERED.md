# Esther: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Why Vashti refused (1:12): the text gives no reason, and only the Student Manual and Keil suggest one; What the clause in 1:22 ("speak according to the language of his people") was meant to secure: no source read explains it.
- **2:** Why Mordecai charged Esther not to make her people known (2:10, 20): no source gives a motive; What form the king's 'release' (hănāḥâ) to the provinces took (2:18): BDB says only 'perhaps holiday-making', and the ancient versions differ.
- **3:** What exactly about being a Jew kept Mordecai from bowing (3:4): the text gives no reason, and the sources give only tentative readings; Whether Haman's silver was ever paid (3:9–11; 7:4).
- **4:** Why Mordecai refused the clothes Esther sent (4:4): the text gives no reason, and no source I read supplies one; Why the fast is three days "night or day" when Esther goes in "on the third day" (4:16; 5:1): no Church or Latter-day Saint source I read addresses the count.
- **5:** Why Esther put off her request to a second banquet: the text gives no reason, and only readings exist.
- **6:** How did the king know that Mordecai was a Jew who sat at the gate, and did he remember that his decree was against the Jews (6:10)? The text does not say, and no Church or Latter-day Saint source takes it up.
- **7:** Why the king went out into the garden (7:7): the text gives no reason. The Student Manual's guess (to check Esther's story) is left out, and no Church source answers it.
- **8:** 8:17: what 'becoming Jews' involved for these people (a change of worship or of allegiance) and how long it lasted. No Church or Latter-day Saint source answers it.
- **9:** What Esther's second day in Shushan (9:13) was for: no Church or Latter-day Saint source read comments on it (already listed in the brief).
- **10:** Why the book ends on a tax (10:1): the text gives no reason, and no Church or Latter-day Saint source comments on it.
