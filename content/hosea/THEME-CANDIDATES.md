# Hosea: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). None is chosen; the author chooses, and gives a separate instruction before any page is developed. With fourteen chapters, a topic that lives in one chapter belongs in that chapter's notes.

## Noticed while setting up (2026-10-09; not yet tested against the chapters)

- **The covenant as a marriage** (1:2; 2:2–23; 3:1–5; 4:12–15; 5:3–7; 9:1): the bride who left, the gifts withdrawn, the wilderness, the betrothal “for ever.” Isaiah's page Zion and Her Husband and Jeremiah 2–3 and Ezekiel 16 are its companions on the site.
- **From a wife to a son** (11:1–4, 8–9; 13:13; 14:3): the book's second picture of the Lord's love, a father who taught Ephraim to walk; “out of Egypt have I called my son” and Matthew 2:15.
- **Names reversed** (1:4–9; 1:10–2:1; 2:22–23): Jezreel, Lo-ruhamah, Lo-ammi; scattered and sown; Romans 9 and 1 Peter 2.
- **The knowledge of God and *hesed*** (2:19–20; 4:1, 6; 5:4; 6:3, 6; 10:12; 12:6; 13:4): what the Lord says is missing and what He asks for.
- **“Return”** (in 14 verses, from 2:7 to 14:7): Israel's false returns and the last true one.
- **Egypt** (in 13 verses): the Exodus remembered and undone, and promised again.

## Reported by chapters 1–14 (not yet sorted)

- (1) Names reversed: 1:4, 6, 9; 1:10–11; 2:1; 2:22–23 (with Rom. 9:25–26).
- (1) The covenant sentence ('my people ... your God') unmade and remade: 1:9; 2:23 (Ex. 6:7; Lev. 26:12).
- (1) One head, David their king: 1:11; 3:5 (Ezek. 37:22–24; Ezekiel's page One Shepherd).
- (1) A second exodus: 1:11; 2:14–15; 11:1; 12:9, 13.
- (1) Not saved by bow or horses: 1:5, 7; 2:18; 14:3.
- (2) The covenant as a marriage: accusation, gifts withdrawn, wilderness, betrothal (2:2–20; with Isaiah 54, Jeremiah 2–3, Ezekiel 16).
- (2) The children's names reversed (2:1, 22–23 with 1:4–11; Romans 9:25–26; 1 Peter 2:10).
- (2) Knowledge of God and hesed (2:8, 19–20 with 4:1, 6:6, 13:4–5).
- (2) The wilderness and the Exodus repeated (2:3, 14–15 with 11:1, 12:9, 13:4–5).
- (2) The gifts of the land: grain, wine, oil (2:8–9, 22 with 7:14, 9:1–2, 14:7).
- (3) The covenant as a marriage: 3:1-3 is its last narrative scene (love commanded again, the buying back, the waiting).
- (3) Return: 3:5 is the first place the book says outright that Israel will return.
- (3) David and the one head: 1:11 and 3:5, with Jer. 30:9 and Ezek. 34 and 37 on the site.
- (3) Things taken away so that Israel will seek the Lord: 2:6-13 (the gifts), 3:4 (king, sacrifice, ephod), 5:15.
- (3) The Lord's goodness and the gifts of the land: 2:8, 22; 3:5; 10:1; 14:5-7.
- (4) Knowledge of God: 4:1, 6 (the Hebrew da'at; also 6:6), with 'know' in 2:20; 5:4; 6:3; 13:4. Chapter 4 is the main statement of its lack.
- (4) The Lord's lawsuit (rib): 4:1-3 and 12:2.
- (4) Priests and leaders held responsible: 4:4-10 (with 5:1; 6:9).
- (4) Beth-aven, Gilgal and the shrines: 4:15 (with 5:8; 9:15; 10:5, 8; 12:11).
- (4) The marriage figure continued as 'whoredom': 4:10-18.
- (4) Truth, mercy and knowledge lost and promised again: 4:1 and 2:19-20.
- (5) Seeking and finding the Lord (the verb baqash): 2:7; 3:5; 5:6, 15; 7:10, with 6:1-3 and 10:12.
- (5) The Lord as healer and the false physicians (the verb rapha): 5:13; 6:1; 7:1; 11:3; 14:3-4.
- (5) Knowing the Lord: 2:20; 4:1, 6; 5:3-4; 6:3, 6; 13:4 (already in the brief).
- (5) The severe images of the Lord and their reversal: 5:10-14 with 6:1-3; 13:7-8 with 14:4-8.
- (5) Assyria as the false help: 5:13; 7:11; 8:9; 10:6; 12:1; 14:3 (already in the brief).
- (6) Knowledge of God: 4:1, 4:6; 6:3, 6:6 (with 'know' in 2:20, 5:4, 13:4).
- (6) Hesed, mercy that lasts or does not: 2:19; 4:1; 6:4, 6:6; 10:12; 12:6.
- (6) Morning cloud and dew: 6:4; 13:3; 14:5.
- (6) Tearing and healing, the lion and the physician: 5:13-14; 6:1; 7:1; 11:3; 13:7-8; 14:4.
- (6) Return: 5:15; 6:1; 6:11; 7:10; 14:1.
- (6) The priests charged: 4:4-9; 5:1; 6:9.
- (7) Healing (5:13; 6:1; 7:1; 11:3; 14:4): the wrong physician, the people's confidence, 'when I would have healed', 'they knew not that I healed them', 'I will heal their backsliding'.
- (7) Not knowing (2:8; 4:1, 6; 5:4; 7:9; 11:3): adds to the existing 'knowledge of God' candidate Israel's ignorance of its own condition.
- (7) Egypt and Assyria (5:13; 7:11, 16; 8:9, 13; 9:3, 6; 11:5, 11; 12:1; 14:3): the dove goes out in 7:11 and comes home in 11:11.
- (7) Redeem (7:13; 13:14): the verb's only two uses in the book, in the same form.
- (7) Call (7:7, 11; 11:1, 2, 7): who calls on whom.
- (8) The knowledge of God: 8:2, 8:4 ('we know thee' / 'I knew it not'), with 4:1, 4:6, 5:3-4, 6:3, 6:6, 13:4.
- (8) Egypt as captivity: 8:13, with 7:11, 9:3, 9:6, 11:1, 11:5.
- (8) Sowing and reaping: 8:7, 10:12-13, 2:22-23; Mosiah 7:30-31; D&C 6:33.
- (8) Things made by hands: 8:4-6, 8:14 ('his Maker'), 13:2, 14:3.
- (8) Kings without the Lord: 7:3-7, 8:4, 8:10, 10:3, 13:10-11.
- (8) The covenant curses of Deuteronomy 28 behind Hosea's threats: 8:1 (28:49), 8:13 (28:68).
- (9) Egypt as the Exodus undone, with Assyria as the new Egypt (9:3, 6; with 8:13; 11:5, 11).
- (9) Ephraim's name and fruit (9:16; 10:1, 13; 14:8), with Bowen and Spendlove.
- (9) Israel's history read against it: Gibeah (9:9; 10:9), Baal-peor (9:10), Gilgal (9:15; 4:15; 12:11).
- (9) The hard sayings against children (9:11-16; 10:14; 13:16).
- (9) Love withdrawn and restored: 'I will love them no more' (9:15) against 3:1, 11:4 and 14:4.
- (9) Scattering and gathering (9:17; 11:10-11).
- (10) Farming as the book's figure for covenant life: sowing and reaping (2:22-23; 8:7; 10:12-13), the heifer and the yoke (4:16; 10:11; 11:4), the vine and its fruit (9:10, 16; 10:1; 14:5-8).
- (10) The things Israel trusted, taken away one by one: king (3:4; 7:7; 8:4; 10:3, 7, 15; 13:10-11), calf (8:5-6; 10:5-6; 13:2), Assyria (5:13; 7:11; 10:6; 14:3), armies (1:7; 10:13; 14:3).
- (10) The Lord coming as rain or dew (6:3; 10:12; 14:5).
- (10) Hosea's lines on the Savior's lips (6:6 in Matthew 9:13 and 12:7; 10:8 in Luke 23:30).
- (10) Hesed, mercy, across the book (2:19; 4:1; 6:4, 6; 10:12; 12:6).
- (11) From a wife to a son: the same complaint, 'did not know that I...', is made of both (2:8; 11:3), with the father's words in 11:1-4, 8-9.
- (11) 'Return': Israel's refusal and the Lord's refusal to return to destroy (11:5 twice, 11:9; 'backsliding' in 11:7 and 14:4; the book's last call at 14:1).
- (11) Egypt: called out, not to return, called out again (11:1, 5, 11; with 8:13 and 9:3).
- (11) The lion: tearing, then calling (5:14; 11:10; 13:7-8).
- (11) The dove: fluttering to Egypt and Assyria, then home from both (7:11; 11:11).
- (11) The call unanswered (11:2, 7).
- (12) Jacob as Israel's mirror: 12:2-6, 12-13 (with Genesis 25, 28, 32, 35 on the site).
- (12) Turn and return (shuv): 12:2, 6, 14; with 5:15-6:1 and 14:1.
- (12) Egypt and Assyria: 12:1 (with 5:13; 7:11; 8:9; 14:3).
- (12) The wilderness as judgment and mercy: 12:9 (with 2:14).
- (12) Prophets as the Lord's means: 12:10, 13 (with 6:5).
- (12) The east wind: 12:1; 13:15.
- (12) Gilgal and Gilead: 12:11 (with 4:15; 6:8; 9:15).
- (12) 'I am the LORD thy God from the land of Egypt': 12:9; 13:4.
- (13) 'Saviour' and 'save', the same Hebrew verb: 1:7; 13:4, 10; 14:3.
- (13) The Lord as lion: 5:14; 11:10; 13:7-8.
- (13) Morning cloud and dew: 6:4; 13:3; 14:5.
- (13) Kings not of the Lord's giving: 7:3-7; 8:4; 10:3, 7, 15; 13:10-11.
- (13) Ephraim the fruitful one: 9:16; 10:1; 13:15; 14:8.
- (13) Egypt and the wilderness as the place of knowing: 2:14-15; 11:1; 12:9; 13:4-5.
- (13) The verb the King James gives as 'redeemed' at 7:13 and 'ransom' at 13:14, its only two uses in Hosea.
- (14) Return (shuv): 14:1, 2, 4, 7 are the last four of its 22 uses in the book, with 'backsliding' (11:7; 14:4) from the same verb.
- (14) Dew: 6:4; 13:3; 14:5 (Israel's fleeting goodness, fleeting Ephraim, then the Lord Himself).
- (14) Healing: 5:13; 6:1; 7:1; 11:3; 14:4.
- (14) The children's names answered: 1:6; 2:1, 23; 14:3 (Lo-ruhamah's verb in the people's prayer).
- (14) Ephraim and fruit: 9:16; 10:1; 13:15; 14:8.
- (14) Stumbling: 4:5; 5:5; 14:1, 9.
- (14) False helps: Assyria and Egypt (5:13; 7:11; 8:9; 12:1; 14:3); the calf and the work of hands (8:6; 13:2; 14:3).
