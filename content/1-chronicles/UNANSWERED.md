# 1 Chronicles: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **2:** Who was Jerioth (2:18), and whose sons does 'her sons' mean? No Latter-day Saint source was found; only Cambridge, which thinks the Hebrew is corrupt; Who was Jesse's eighth son, whom Chronicles leaves out (2:13–15)? Neither book says.
- **3:** Why Shelomith, a daughter, is named among Zerubbabel's children (3:19): no source read answers it; Why verse 22 says 'six' when it names five sons: Cambridge only notes the gap and does not explain it.
- **4:** Who "Pharaoh" was in 4:18, and how his daughter came to marry into Judah: no source read says; What the "ancient things" of 4:22 are: the brief lists this as a reader's question, and the chapter has no note on it.
- **5:** Why is Gad's register dated to both Jotham of Judah and Jeroboam of Israel (5:17)? Keil and Cambridge disagree on whether the two reigns overlapped, and no Church source addresses it; Where were Hara and the other places of exile (5:26)? Only Cambridge says Hara is unknown; it is not used; Can the plunder figures of 5:21 (100,000 people) be right? Nothing specific to this verse was found; the site's general note on large numbers is at 2 Sam. 24:9.
- **6:** Why does the high priests' line appear a second time (verses 50–53), stopping at Ahimaaz? No source read gives a reason; the note only says the chapter repeats it after drawing the line between Levites and priests; Why does verse 57 list eleven priestly cities by name when verse 60 totals thirteen (Josh. 21 has Juttah and Gibeon)? The writer did not answer this and no source was read for it.
- **7:** Why does 1 Chr. 7:15 call Zelophehad 'the second' when Num. 26:29–33 places him three generations below Machir? No source read for this chapter explains it; Whose wife is 'she' in 'Ashriel, whom she bare' (7:14)? The verse does not say, and no source was read for it.
- **8:** Where was Manahath? Keil says the place is not known. The chapter does not answer it.
- **10:** 10:13: no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source found that says which 'word of the LORD' Saul did not keep. Only the Cambridge Bible names 1 Sam. 13 and 15.
- **16:** Who served as priest before the ark in Jerusalem while Zadok was at Gibeon (Abiathar?): only Keil and the Cambridge Bible guess, and the writer left it out.
- **17:** Why Chronicles words the promise of 17:11–14 differently from 2 Sam. 7 ("of thy sons", no rod, "mine house and my kingdom"): no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source explains it. The only explanation found was Keil's Messianic reading, which I cut.
- **19:** Why the hired army camped at Medeba (19:7): no source says; Why Chronicles reads 'came upon them' (19:17) where Samuel has 'came to Helam': no source explains the difference in the Hebrew.
- **23:** Why 38,000 Levites here, against the 8,580 of Num. 4:48 (the writer left this out); What the Levites' 'officers and judges' (23:4) did.
- **26:** What Samuel and Saul dedicated (26:28): no source read says.
- **27:** Why is Benaiah called 'a chief priest' (27:5)?; Why are an Ishmaelite and a Hagerite over the king's camels and flocks (27:30–31)?; Was Jonathan, 'David's uncle' (27:32), an uncle or a nephew?; Why do Gad and Asher have no prince (27:16–22)?.
