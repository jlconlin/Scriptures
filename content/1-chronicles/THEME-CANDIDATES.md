### 1. “A Man of Rest” (from [[1 Chr. 22:9]])

**The arc.** Chronicles leaves out the rest 2 Samuel gives David (17:1, 10, against 2 Sam. 7:1, 11). David wins the wars and dedicates their spoil to the house (18:8, 11), but the wars are why he may not build it: “thou hast shed blood abundantly” (22:8). The son will be “a man of rest” (*ʾîš mĕnûḥâ*, 22:9); David meant to build “an house of rest” (*bêt mĕnûḥâ*) and was refused as “a man of war” (28:2–3). When the rest comes, the Levites who carried the tabernacle lay the burden down and are given praise instead (23:25–30; 6:31–32); then “arise, O LORD God, into thy resting place” (2 Chr. 6:41).

**Thesis.** The work of the LORD's anointed is divided between two men because no mortal could hold both halves: the one who shed blood to win the rest could not build the house of rest, and the one who built it had shed none. Jesus Christ is the one Son of David in whom both meet, because the blood by which He made peace was His own.

**To Christ:** [[Col. 1:20]]; [[Eph. 2:13–22]]; [[Zech. 6:12–13]]; [[Matt. 11:28–30]], [[Matt. 12:6]], [[Matt. 12:42]]; [[Isa. 9:6–7]]. **Applies:** [[Matt. 11:28–29]]; [[John 14:27]]; [[1 Cor. 3:16–17]]; [[1 Pet. 2:5]]; the Levites' change from bearing to praise is the change His yoke makes.

**Must not claim:** that David's wars were sins (22:18 credits them with the rest; the note at 22:8 gives Skinner's reading as his); that war is foreign to the LORD without facing “The LORD is a man of war” ([[Ex. 15:3]], the phrase of 28:3); anything about Isa. 11:10's root of Jesse beyond D&C 113:5–6 exactly; a reason for the omission at 17:1 beyond the older commentaries' (the note there). No Church source calls Solomon a type in this respect: the page says once that the reading is its own. Hebrews 3–4 belongs to Joshua's *Rest Round About*: link, don't repeat.

## Not chosen

“The Throne of the LORD” (17:14; 28:5; 29:23; Matt. 22:45: the note at 28:5 already gathers most of it); the census and the Good Shepherd (2 Samuel's *Would God I Had Died for Thee* has it); the genealogies, the Levites' institutions, the threshing floor, a perfect heart, the breach, rest as such, Jabez. Each is carried by the chapter notes, stays in one place, or is already another book's page. Seeking the LORD is part of 2 Chronicles' chosen page.
