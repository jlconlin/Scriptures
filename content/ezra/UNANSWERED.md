# Ezra: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Who was Mithredath the treasurer (1:8), and what does 'prince' (nāśîʾ) of Judah mean for Sheshbazzar's office? The writer left both unanswered.
- **2:** Why so many priests (4,289) returned and so few Levites (74): no source read explains it; What a 'dram' of gold was (a daric?): only Keil says so; Bible Hub has no BDB entry for H1871.
- **4:** Whether Artaxerxes' order in 4:21 was later lifted by the letters he gave Nehemiah (Neh. 2:7–8): no source read connects the two.
- **6:** 6:20: why the Levites, not the priests, killed the Passover for everyone, priests included. No note answers it; 2 Chr. 30:17 and 35:11 are where to look; 6:3: why the decree's sixty-cubit height and breadth go beyond Solomon's house (1 Kgs. 6:2) while the Church's sources call the temple about the same size. No source read explains it.
- **7:** 7:12: what 'perfect peace, and at such a time' means. The writer left it without a source; 7:14: who the king's 'seven counsellors' were. The writer left it without a source.
- **8:** Where Ahava and Casiphia lay, and what kind of community Iddo led at Casiphia (8:15–17); Why the Levites were so few or so slow to come (8:15); no Church or Latter-day Saint source gives a reason.
- **10:** What became of the wives and children sent away (10:44): no source read says; Why Jonathan and Jahaziah objected (10:15): the text does not say, and no source read explains it.
