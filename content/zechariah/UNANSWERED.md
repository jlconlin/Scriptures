# Zechariah: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 1:8: what the colours of the horses mean (no Church or Latter-day Saint source explains them; Keil calls the third colour disputed).
- **2:** 2:13: what "raised up out of his holy habitation" signifies here. No source the chapter uses answers it, now that the Deut. 26:15 connection is cut.
- **3:** Zech 3:9: what the 'graving' (engraving) on the stone is. No source read answers it, so it is not in the chapter.
- **5:** Zech. 5:9: why the carriers are two women with stork's wings. No Church or Latter-day Saint source explains it; only Keil does, and he was cut to stay within the limit on outside commentary; Zech. 5:7: why wickedness appears as a woman. The text doesn't say; the Student Manual identifies her without explaining why the figure is a woman.
- **6:** Why the red team of 6:2 gets no mission in 6:6–7. Only Keil discusses it, and he weighs emendations; left out as minor.
- **7:** Who Sherezer and Regem-melech were (7:2): no source read identifies them beyond the verse.
- **9:** 9:1: where exactly was Hadrach? BDB says only 'perhaps' an Assyrian district near Damascus and Hamath; 9:15: what 'filled like bowls, and as the corners of the altar' and 'subdue with sling stones' mean. No note covers these verses; the obvious reader question goes unanswered.
- **10:** Why are Egypt and Assyria named in a prophecy of about 520 B.C., when Assyria had already fallen? No source read says when it fell; Who exactly were the shepherds and he-goats of 10:3: Judah's own leaders or foreign governors? The Church's sources don't say.
- **11:** Who the three shepherds of 11:8 were (no source names them); What covenant 'with all the people' is broken in 11:10: Scripture Helps reads it as a covenant between God and His people, Keil as one made with the nations; the chapter gives only Scripture Helps' reading; Who brings the fire on Lebanon in 11:1–3, and when.
- **12:** 12:12–14: why the wives mourn apart from their husbands. Keil quotes the verses without explaining it, and Correa does not discuss it; 12:11: no Church source addresses whether Hadadrimmon is a place or a deity. The chapter follows Keil's reading, the mourning for Josiah, given as the likeliest.
- **13:** 13:8: who the 'two parts' cut off are, and when. The only source read (Keil) dates it to the Jewish wars and later persecutions, which the writer left out under the brief's caution against timetables; 13:2: what 'the unclean spirit' is. Keil's reading was cut to keep Keil within the brief's limit, and no Church or Latter-day Saint source read addresses it.
- **14:** 14:5: where Azal was. No source read places it other than Correa's 'behind the Mount of Olives'; 14:6: what the obscure Hebrew of verse 6 means. Only Keil's reading was found.
