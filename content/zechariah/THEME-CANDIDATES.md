# Zechariah: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). **None is written until the author chooses it.** The candidates came from Fable's and Codex's lists (`.cache/batch/zechariah/reports/themes-fable.md` and `themes-codex.md`, the second after Codex was run again with the chapters), shown to the author as one combined list (`AUTHORING.md`, “Theme candidates at the end of a book”); only the author's choice is recorded here.

## Author's selection (2026-10-23)

**Three pages are chosen** (author, 2026-10-23: “I approve Z1-Z3”):

- **Z1. “Turn ye unto me, and I will turn unto you”** (Fable's first, with Codex's “I am returned to Jerusalem with mercies”; both lists' first). The promise on both sides (1:3); the Lord turns first, “returned to Jerusalem with mercies” (1:16; 8:3); the people's cry unanswered because they would not hear (7:13); the spirit of supplications poured out (12:10) and 7:13 reversed, “they shall call on my name, and I will hear them” (13:9), after the Shepherd is smitten (13:7); the Lord king over all the earth (14:9). To Christ: Matt. 26:31; D&C 88:63 (the footnote at 1:3). The angel of the LORD pleading in 1:12 may be set beside D&C 45:3–5 only as the page's comparison, never identified with Christ.
- **Z2. “Awake, O sword, against my shepherd”** (Fable's third, with Codex's shepherd page, “by the blood of thy covenant” and Judah and Ephraim). The flock without a shepherd (10:2); the shepherd priced at thirty pieces and the staffs Beauty and Bands broken (11:7–14); the sword on the worthless shepherd (11:17), then on the Lord's own (13:7); “It is my people” restated (13:9). To Christ: Matt. 26:31 and 26:28 the same night; Heb. 13:20 joins shepherd and covenant blood. 9:11 is not said to name Christ's blood without a source. The “three shepherds” of 11:8 are not identified.
- **Z3. “HOLINESS UNTO THE LORD”: from one clean priest to every pot** (Fable's fifth, with Codex's cleansing arc and the mourning of 12:10 from Fable's fasts proposal). Joshua's filthy garments taken away and the clean turban (3:3–5; Ex. 28:36); the land's iniquity removed in one day (3:9); wickedness carried out (5); the mourning for the pierced one and the fountain opened (12:10; 13:1); the high priest's words on the horses' bells and every pot holy (14:20–21). To Christ: John 19:37; D&C 45:51–53; Rev. 7:14; 1 Pet. 2:9. It answers Haggai's theme page (“Shall It Be Holy?”: holiness does not pass by touch), and the two pages link to each other.

Left out of the combined list: the Branch (the notes on chapters 3–6 carry it), “the day of small things” (one list only; judged to rest on similarity), “at evening time it shall be light” (within chapter 14), the fasts as a page of their own (the mourning of 12:10 went into Z3).
