## The arm of the Lord: three chapter notes say more than the sources do (2026-10-01)

Research for the theme page found no scripture, Church reference work, or manual that says in so many words that the arm of the Lord *is* Jesus Christ. Church manuals call the arm a symbol of the Lord's power; John 12:37–41, Abinadi (Mosiah 12–15), D&C 29:1 and D&C 133:46–51 show that power revealed in, and wielded by, Christ. Latter-day Saint writers differ on Isaiah 53:1 (*Isaiah in the Book of Mormon*, p. 496). The theme page keeps to that. Three existing notes go further, in the site's own voice:

- **Isaiah 53:1, “Who would have believed it?”:** “Now the arm is revealed, and it is the man described in the following verses.”
- **Isaiah 52:10, “The arm made bare”:** “The Savior presents Himself as the Father’s arm, the instrument by which the Father saves” and “The arm, when it is finally bared, is a disfigured man.” (The note on 51:5 has the same thought.)
- **Isaiah 59:16, “No intercessor”:** says 63:5 repeats the scene “almost word for word.” (The Church footnotes do point each verse to the other.)

**Question for the author:** reword these to what the sources say (for example, “the arm is revealed in the servant’s suffering,” attributed where it is a scholar’s reading) and link them to the theme page, or leave them as they are?
