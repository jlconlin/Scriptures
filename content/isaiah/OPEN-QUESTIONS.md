## Chapter notes that say more than their sources (found during theme research, 2026-10-02)

The research and reviews for the theme pages turned up sentences in existing chapters that go further than any source read. The theme pages keep to the sources; these chapters were left alone.

- **Isaiah 54, `christ` and line 12:** “Here those children arrive… and at the end a new name for them, ‘the servants of the LORD’” and “what the servant’s suffering produces: a family” identify the servants with the servant’s seed. No scripture or Church source does; only Delitzsch ties them, and he says the ideas “do not cover one another.” (The “new name” is from 65:15, not 54:17.)
- **Isaiah 56 and 65:** notes say the single servant “has become” or “gives way to” a community.
- **Isaiah 60:1:** “His light first, then ours.” **Isaiah 60:19** (no sources listed): “the two halves of Isaiah meet here.” **Isaiah 10:17:** “a paradox Isaiah returns to often.”
- **Isaiah 62:10:** the note lays out the highway passages as a sequence and lists no sources.

**Question for the author:** reword these to what the sources say and link them to the theme pages, as was done for the arm-of-the-Lord notes, or leave them?
