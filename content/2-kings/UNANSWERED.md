# 2 Kings: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **2:** Why Elisha went on to Carmel and then to Samaria (2:25): only an aside in the Old Testament Minute touches it.
- **4:** Why did Elisha tell the widow to shut the door (4:4)? Only Keil addresses it; Why did the child sneeze seven times (4:35)?; Why did the LORD hide the child's death from Elisha (4:27)?.
- **7:** Whether the noise of chariots (7:6) was something the Syrians actually heard or a confusion of their senses: only Keil addresses it, and it was left out; A Latter-day Saint reading of 'this day is a day of good tidings' (7:9) as sharing the gospel: the writer found no source.
- **10:** Is Jehu known outside the Bible (the Black Obelisk of Shalmaneser III, v. 34)? The British Museum page was blocked and the BYU Religious Studies Center pages loaded only as stubs, according to the writer's report.
- **13:** Why did Joash stop at three strokes (13:18)? No Latter-day Saint source gives a reason; only Keil does; How did Jehoahaz's and Jehoash's reign figures (13:1, 10) come to disagree? No Latter-day Saint source reconciles them.
- **15:** Where was Tiphsah (15:16), and why would Menahem attack it from Tirzah? The chapter has no source on it, and the plain paraphrase now keeps the King James wording.
- **16:** Why Ahaz cut the panels from the stands and moved the sea 'for the king of Assyria' (16:17–18): the sources give only conjectures, and Keil says no link with Assyria can be proved.
- **17:** Who were Succoth-benoth, Nergal, Ashima, Nibhaz, Tartak, Adrammelech and Anammelech (17:30–31)? Only Cambridge treats them, and the writer cut them as a curiosity.
- **19:** What were 'the rivers of besieged places' (v.24) and 'a blast' (v.7)? Isaiah 37 has no note on either, and this chapter leaves them to Isaiah under the brief; Who or what were Gozan, Haran, Rezeph, Eden/Thelasar, Hena and Ivah (vv.12–13)? Neither this chapter nor Isaiah 37 has a note.
- **20:** Why did Hezekiah ask for a sign on the dial of Ahaz, when Ahaz had refused a sign (Isa. 7:11–12)? The writer found no source that makes the connection.
- **24:** 8: Jehoiachin is 'eighteen years old' here but 'eight' in 2 Chr. 36:9 (footnote 8a); the chapter does not address the difference; 17: Mattaniah is Jehoiachin's 'father's brother' here but 'his brother' in 2 Chr. 36:10 (footnote 17a); the chapter does not address the difference.
