# 2 Samuel: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 2 Sam. 1:26: what David meant by comparing Jonathan's love with 'the love of women'. No faithful source read explains the comparison, so the note gives David's words and the covenant only.
- **4:** Why did the Beerothites flee to Gittaim (4:3)? The only answer found is an older commentary's guess; Why are the murder and its details told twice in 4:6–7? The note gives only the Septuagint's different telling, and no Latter-day Saint source explains it.
- **5:** Which 'hold' David went down to in 5:17 (only Keil identifies it); Whether the forty years of 5:4 is a round number, and why 5:4–5 is missing from 4QSam-a (Correa only; the writer cut it as peripheral).
- **6:** What Nachon's threshingfloor was (6:6); The fir-wood instruments of 6:5.
- **10:** What kindness had Nahash shown David (10:2)? Scripture does not say, and Keil only guesses.
- **11:** Why does 1 Chr. 20:1 tell of the siege of Rabbah but leave out the Bath-sheba story? (The writer found no source that answers it.).
- **12:** v. 30: how a crown could weigh a talent, and whether it was Milcom's (only an older commentary takes it up; left to the plain paraphrase); v. 14: what 'given great occasion to the enemies of the LORD to blaspheme' means beyond the text's own clause.
