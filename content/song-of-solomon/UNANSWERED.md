# Song of Solomon: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **2:** 2:14: why he pictures her as a dove in the rock. The only source that answered it was Delitzsch, on her being out of reach, and he is already cited in a third of the notes; 2:17: whether 'the day breathes' means morning or evening, and whether 'turn' means come back or go away. No source the chapter keeps answers either.
- **3:** 3:11: Whom Solomon marries, and why his mother crowns him: no source read says.
- **5:** 5:10–16: what the costly materials (gold, beryl, ivory, sapphires) meant to an ancient hearer. The only source is the older commentary, and the writer left it out to keep that commentary to a third of the notes; 5:6: why the beloved withdrew. No source read gives a reason.
- **6:** Why the poem compares her to Tirzah in particular (no Church, BYU or Scripture Central source says; Delitzsch's reading was cut to keep his notes within the brief's limit); What 'the dance of Mahanaim' was to the first hearers.
