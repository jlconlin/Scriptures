# Habakkuk: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 1:9: 'their faces shall sup up as the east wind': no source read gives a clear reading; 1:7: 'their judgment and their dignity shall proceed of themselves': only the plain-words section deals with it; no note.
- **2:** 2:17: what are 'the violence of Lebanon' and 'the spoil of beasts'? Only Keil treats it, and the chapter has no note; 2:10: what does 'sinned against thy soul' mean? Not treated.
- **3:** 3:5: what the pestilence and burning coals before God signify (no source read goes beyond the footnote "HEB a flame"); 3:9: "according to the oaths of the tribes, even thy word" (obscure; only the plain-words section addresses it).
