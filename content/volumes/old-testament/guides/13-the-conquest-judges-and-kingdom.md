This guide covers Israel's settlement of the land under Joshua and the judges, and the united kingdom of Saul, David and Solomon. It ends where Solomon's death divided the kingdom in two;[@scripture-helps-1-kings-24] the story goes on in [Two Kingdoms and Assyria](/old-testament/guides/two-kingdoms-and-assyria/). For the whole Old Testament, see [A Timeline of the Old Testament](/old-testament/guides/israels-story/#judges) and its section on [the united kingdom](/old-testament/guides/israels-story/#united-kingdom).

The dates are uncertain. The judges' dates are “very uncertain,”[@bible-chron-ot] and no Church reference dates the conquest; one dating places it “between 1250 and 1200 B.C.”[@ot-manual-judges-1-12] For the kings, the Bible Chronology gives Saul 1095, David in Jerusalem 1047, Solomon 1015 and the division 975.[@bible-chron-ot] Other Church pages say David took Jerusalem about 1000,[@ot-manual-2-samuel-1] begin Rehoboam and Jeroboam in 931,[@ot-manual-kings-a] and put David's death around 968.[@scripture-helps-1-kings-24] The table gives each date as its source does.

<h2 id="books">The books of this period</h2>

- Joshua: the conquest (1–12), then the allotment of the land and Joshua's last words (13–24).[@bd-joshua-book]
- Judges: compiled long after the events;[@bd-judges] with Ruth, the history from Joshua's death to Samuel's birth.[@bd-judges]
- Ruth: a family from Bethlehem in the days of the judges, and David's ancestors.[@bd-ruth]
- 1 and 2 Samuel: one book in Hebrew, from Samuel's birth nearly to David's death, about 130 years.[@bd-samuel-books]
- 1 Kings 1–11: the reign of Solomon, the temple, and his turning to false gods.[@scripture-helps-1-kings-24]
- 1 Chronicles 10–2 Chronicles 9: Saul's death ([[1 Chr. 10:6]]) to Solomon's death ([[2 Chr. 9:31]]); Chronicles cites sources such as “the book of Nathan the prophet.”[@bd-chronicles]
- Psalms: seventy-three are ascribed to David.[@bd-psalms]
