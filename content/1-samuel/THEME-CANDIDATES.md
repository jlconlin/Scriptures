# 1 Samuel: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). **None is written until the author chooses it.** The candidates were Fable's and Codex's lists (`.cache/batch/1-samuel/reports/themes-fable.md` and `themes-codex.md`), shown to the author combined with 2 Samuel's on 2026-11-05.

## Author's selection (2026-11-05)

**No theme page for 1 Samuel.** The three candidates shown, “How Shall This Man Save Us?” (the verb “save” through the book), “Them That Honour Me I Will Honour” (2:30 tested on Eli, Saul and David) and “Who Shall Entreat for Him?” (Eli's question and Samuel's intercession), were not chosen. The author: “The others don't seem to feel like a real connection; it seems like they are too disconnected.” 2 Samuel's “Would God I Had Died for Thee” was chosen instead (`content/2-samuel/THEME-CANDIDATES.md`).
