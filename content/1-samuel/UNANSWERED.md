# 1 Samuel: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **5:** What the loss of Dagon's head and hands signifies (5:4): no source read says; Why the lords chose Gath and then Ekron (5:8, 10): Keil's guess was not used, and no Latter-day Saint source answers it.
- **6:** 6:20: why the men of Beth-shemesh sent the ark away rather than keeping it in a priests' city. No Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source read takes it up; Why the ark went to Kirjath-jearim and not back to Shiloh. The text does not say, and only an older commentary remarks on it.
- **7:** What the twenty years of 7:2 count, when the ark stays at Kirjath-jearim until 2 Samuel 6: only Keil and the Cambridge Bible answer (cut 2026-11-04)
- **9:** How the LORD's sending a king “to save my people” (9:16) squares with His displeasure at the asking (8:7): only Keil answers (cut 2026-11-04)
- **10:** Why Saul hid among the stuff (10:22): no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source found; only older commentaries; What the bread given at Bethel's pilgrims meant (10:4): only older commentaries; Why Samuel kissed Saul (10:1): only two older commentaries, which disagree.; What Samuel's kiss meant (10:1), and why a lot was cast for a man already anointed (10:20): only Keil and the Cambridge Bible answer (cut 2026-11-04)
- **12:** 12:23: why Samuel counts it a sin to stop praying for the people. No Church, BYU or Scripture Central source read answers it; the only source read, Keil, turns it into a lesson for rulers.
- **14:** Why did Jonathan not tell his father (14:1)? Only Cambridge guesses; Who were the "Hebrews that were with the Philistines" (14:21)? Only Cambridge answers; Why does Chronicles list other sons of Saul (14:49)? Only Cambridge answers.
- **16:** Why does 1 Chr. 2:13–15 list seven sons of Jesse, with David the seventh, when 16:10 implies eight? No source in the hierarchy was read on it.
- **17:** 17:8–10: why did armies settle a battle by single combat? Scripture Helps answers this beside the verse, so the chapter rightly does not repeat it.
- **18:** Why does 18:16 say "Israel and Judah"? No source read explains it beyond the footnote pointer to 2 Sam. 2:4.
- **19:** Where is Sechu (19:22)? No source locates it; Why did Michal say David threatened her ('why should I kill thee', 19:17)? Only Keil gives a reading, and the chapter does not use it.
- **20:** 20:16: what 'Let the LORD even require it at the hand of David's enemies' means; only Keil discusses it; 20:41: why David bowed three times; only Cambridge discusses it; 20:19: what the stone Ezel was; only Cambridge and Keil discuss it.
- **21:** Why Mark 2:26 names Abiathar as the high priest when 1 Sam. 21 names Ahimelech; Why Ahimelech 'was afraid at the meeting of David' (21:1). Only Cambridge guesses; What 'detained before the LORD' means for Doeg (21:7). Only Keil and Cambridge answer it.
- **23:** 23:12: was the LORD's answer that Keilah would give David up conditional on his staying? The text and Keil say only that both answers were yes and David left before Saul acted; 23:27–28: where the Philistine raid was, and what Sela-hammahlekoth means beyond the footnote's "rock of divisions." Only Keil discusses it, and his rendering differs from the footnote.
- **24:** Why does David call Saul 'my father' (24:11)? No source read says; Is chapter 26 a second telling of the same story? No source in the hierarchy addresses it.
