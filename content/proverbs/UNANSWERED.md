# Proverbs: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 1:23: what “my spirit” means in Wisdom's mouth, beyond BDB's “divine spirit”; no source read explains it.
- **10:** Does any Church or Latter-day Saint source say that 1 Pet. 4:8 and James 5:20 take up Prov. 10:12? Searches of the Religious Studies Center, the KnoWhys and the Church's footnotes found none; Is the rich man's "strong city" (10:15) praise of wealth or only an observation? Only Delitzsch answers it. The note was cut.
- **11:** 11:7: does the line imply hope beyond death for the righteous, as the Greek suggests? No source the writer read answered it; 11:30: no Latter-day Saint source was found that reads 'winneth souls' of missionary work.
- **12:** 12:26: what 'more excellent than his neighbour' means (the NRSVUE marks the Hebrew uncertain); no Latter-day Saint source found; 12:27: the sense of 'roasteth not that which he took in hunting'; the Hebrew is uncertain and no Latter-day Saint source covers it.
- **14:** 14:32: no Church or Latter-day Saint source was found on 'the righteous hath hope in his death' or on the 'in his integrity' reading of the Greek and Syriac. The note rests on Delitzsch, as one older reading; 14:10 ('a stranger doth not intermeddle with his joy'): no source was found on what 'intermeddle' meant in the King James.
- **16:** 16:6: whose 'mercy and truth' purge iniquity, the person's or God's? No Church or Latter-day Saint source read answers it; only Delitzsch does; 16:4: no Church source read comments on 'even the wicked for the day of evil'.
- **17:** v. 10: why 'an hundred stripes'? No source read goes beyond the word; v. 12: why a bear robbed of her whelps? No source read answers it beyond the plain image.
- **18:** 18:21: who is the 'it' in 'they that love it'? Is it the tongue, or death and life? No Church or Latter-day Saint source read answers this.
- **20:** 20:27 'The spirit of man is the candle of the LORD': no Latter-day Saint source was found in this session reading it with the Light of Christ (none was searched for in review).
- **22:** 22:6: no Church or Latter-day Saint source read discusses what the Hebrew 'according to his way' means. Only Delitzsch does, and he rejects the King James sense; 22:20: no source read says the emendation to 'thirty' was made because of Amenemope; the sources only note that the section has about thirty parts.
