The Hebrew noun for “vats” (the Church's edition footnotes “fats” as “vats”) and the verb “overflow” stand together twice in Joel, at [[Joel 2:24]] and [[Joel 3:13]], and nowhere else in the book.[@oshb-wlc] The lexicon sets the two verses side by side: “the vats overflow with must, so … Joel 4:13,” which is 3:13 in the King James numbering.[@bdb] In 2:24 they overflow with wine and oil. In 3:13 nothing is named as filling the vats; the nations' wickedness is given as the reason they overflow. Around those two verses the book's wine is lost and then promised back.

| Step | Passage | The words |
|---|---|---|
| Lost | [[Joel 1:5]], [[Joel 1:9–10]] | “it is cut off from your mouth” |
| Promised | [[Joel 2:19]], [[Joel 2:24]] | “the fats shall overflow with wine and oil” |
| Judged | [[Joel 3:13]] | “the press is full, the fats overflow” |
| Followed to Christ | [[D&C 76:107]], [[Rev. 14:19]] | “trodden the wine-press alone” |
| Returned | [[Joel 3:18]] | “the mountains shall drop down new wine” |

## “Cut off from your mouth”

“Awake, ye drunkards, and weep; and howl, all ye drinkers of wine, because of the new wine; for it is cut off from your mouth” ([[Joel 1:5]]). The loss is at the altar too: “The meat offering and the drink offering is cut off from the house of the LORD” ([[Joel 1:9]]). The verb is the same root in both verses, and again of food in [[Joel 1:16]].[@oshb-wlc] The new wine “is dried up” ([[Joel 1:10]]). See the note at [[Joel 1:9]].

## “The fats shall overflow”

After the call to turn and the fast, the LORD answers: “Behold, I will send you corn, and wine, and oil, and ye shall be satisfied therewith” ([[Joel 2:19]]). Then: “the floors shall be full of wheat, and the fats shall overflow with wine and oil” ([[Joel 2:24]]). See the note at [[Joel 2:25]].

## “The press is full, the fats overflow”

“Put ye in the sickle, for the harvest is ripe: come, get you down; for the press is full, the fats overflow; for their wickedness is great” ([[Joel 3:13]]). The noun and the verb are the same as in 2:24: a promise there (“shall overflow”), a present fact here. The verb “full” is also the one used of the floors in 2:24.[@oshb-wlc] This time no wine is named; what the verse gives is a reason, “their wickedness is great.” The verse stands inside the judgment of the nations gathered to the valley ([[Joel 3:12]]), and the wickedness is theirs; it is not a second vintage in Judah. **Connection made by this page:** set side by side, 2:24 and 3:13 are two different things in one set of words: a harvest given, and a judgment pictured as a harvest.[@lds-scriptures] See the note at [[Joel 3:13]].

## Who treads

The Church's footnote on “press” sends the reader to [[Rev. 14:19]] and [[D&C 76:107]].[@lds-scriptures] In John's vision the harvest and the vine are two reapings. One “like unto the Son of man” reaps the earth ([[Rev. 14:14–16]]), and the footnote there reads “TG Jesus Christ, Son of Man.”[@lds-scriptures] An angel then gathers the vine into “the great winepress of the wrath of God,” and “the winepress was trodden” without a treader being named ([[Rev. 14:19–20]]).

Other passages name one who treads a winepress, and the footnote on “winepress” at Rev. 14:19 lists two of them with Joel 3:13.[@lds-scriptures] The rider called “The Word of God” “treadeth the winepress of the fierceness and wrath of Almighty God” ([[Rev. 19:13]], [[Rev. 19:15]]).[@lds-scriptures] Christ, when He delivers up the kingdom to the Father, says, “I have overcome and have trodden the wine-press alone” ([[D&C 76:107]]); “The Lamb of God hath overcome and trodden the wine-press alone” ([[D&C 88:106]]).[@lds-scriptures] And Joel himself says who judges: “there will I sit to judge” ([[Joel 3:12]]), where the footnote is “TG Jesus Christ, Judge.”[@lds-scriptures] For the treading, see the note at [[Isa. 63:3]].

## “The mountains shall drop down new wine”

“The mountains shall drop down new wine, and the hills shall flow with milk … and a fountain shall come forth of the house of the LORD” ([[Joel 3:18]]). The Hebrew word for “new wine” here is the one cut off in 1:5, and Joel uses it only in those two verses. The “new wine” of 1:10 is a different word, the one translated “wine” in 2:19 and 2:24.[@oshb-wlc] **Connection made by this page:** set beside each other, the new wine cut off in 1:5 and the new wine dripping in 3:18 are loss and return.[@oshb-wlc] This is not the wine of the press in 3:13, which names none. See the note at [[Joel 3:18]].

## Through Jesus Christ

Jehovah, the name the King James text prints as LORD, is the premortal Jesus Christ.[@bd-jehovah] **Connection made by this page:** set beside each other, the LORD who says “I will send you corn, and wine, and oil” ([[Joel 2:19]]) is the one who says “there will I sit to judge” ([[Joel 3:12]]), and the vats overflow in the verses that follow each ([[Joel 2:24]]; [[Joel 3:13]]).[@lds-scriptures] The scriptures above name Him as the one who has trodden the winepress.
