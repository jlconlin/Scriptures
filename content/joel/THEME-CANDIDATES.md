# Joel: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). **The author has selected none**; nothing here is developed until the author says so. With three chapters, most of what recurs is better carried by one note and two pointers than by a page.

## Noticed while setting up (2026-10-09; not yet tested against the chapters)

- **The day of the Lord** (1:15; 2:1, 11, 31; 3:14): near and dark in every use, and by the end “the LORD will be the hope of his people” (3:16). Its companions on the site are `[[Isa. 13:6]]` and `[[Mal. 4:5]]`; the Doctrine and Covenants takes up its signs (29:14; 45:40–42).
- **“I will pour out my spirit”** (2:28–32): Joel, Peter at Pentecost (Acts 2), Paul (Romans 10:13), Moroni to Joseph Smith (Joseph Smith—History 1:41). An arc that leaves the book almost at once; it may be one note and not a page.
- **What the locust ate, and what is restored** (1:4–20; 2:19–27; 3:18): wine, grain, oil, and the offerings cut off; then rain, full floors, and a fountain from the house of the Lord.
