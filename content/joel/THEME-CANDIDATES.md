# Joel: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). **The author has selected none**; nothing here is developed until the author says so. With three chapters, most of what recurs is better carried by one note and two pointers than by a page.

## Noticed while setting up (2026-10-09; not yet tested against the chapters)

- **The day of the Lord** (1:15; 2:1, 11, 31; 3:14): near and dark in every use, and by the end “the LORD will be the hope of his people” (3:16). Its companions on the site are `[[Isa. 13:6]]` and `[[Mal. 4:5]]`; the Doctrine and Covenants takes up its signs (29:14; 45:40–42).
- **“I will pour out my spirit”** (2:28–32): Joel, Peter at Pentecost (Acts 2), Paul (Romans 10:13), Moroni to Joseph Smith (Joseph Smith—History 1:41). An arc that leaves the book almost at once; it may be one note and not a page.
- **What the locust ate, and what is restored** (1:4–20; 2:19–27; 3:18): wine, grain, oil, and the offerings cut off; then rain, full floors, and a fountain from the house of the Lord.

## Reported by chapters 1–3 (not yet sorted)

- (1) The day of the Lord (1:15; 2:1, 11, 31; 3:14): the full note is at 1:15.
- (1) What the locust ate and what is restored (1:4, 9-12, 16-20; answered at 2:14, 2:19-27): the offering cut off and hoped for again, the beasts answered at 2:22, the four insects in another order at 2:25.
- (1) The fast and solemn assembly (1:14; 2:15), with D&C 88:70 and 95:7.
- (2) The day of the Lord: 2:1, 2:11, 2:31 (with 1:15; 3:14).
- (2) Pouring, first rain and then the Spirit: 2:23, 2:28–29.
- (2) What the locust ate and what is repaid: 2:14, 2:19, 2:24–26 (with 1:9–12; 3:18).
- (2) Zion and the holy mountain: 2:1, 2:15, 2:23, 2:32 (with 3:16–17, 21).
- (2) The darkened sun and moon: 2:10, 2:31 (with 3:15); D&C 45:40–42.
- (3) The day of the Lord: 1:15; 2:1, 11, 31; 3:14 (fifth and last use), with 3:16 as its other side, "the hope of his people".
- (3) What was lost and what is restored: 3:18 uses the Hebrew words of 1:5 (new wine) and 1:20 (streambeds), and the house of the Lord of 1:9; 3:13 reuses the overflowing vats of 2:24.
- (3) "Sanctify": 1:14; 2:15, 16 (the fast and the congregation); 3:9 (the nations' war).
- (3) Zion, the holy mountain, and the Lord dwelling there: 2:1, 32; 3:16, 17, 21 (the verb "dwell" only in 3:17 and 3:21).
- (3) "Ye shall know that I am the LORD your God": 2:27 and 3:17.
