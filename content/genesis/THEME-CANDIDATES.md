# Genesis: theme candidates

Topics that run across chapters, reported as chapters are written. Most will never become pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8, “What earns a theme page”). No Genesis theme page is written until the author chooses it. References marked “untested” were not read in the session that reported them.

- **The Abrahamic covenant, from the call to the oath and on to Joseph's death.** All nations blessed in the seed (12:3; 22:18); the oath God swears by Himself (22:16) and its later recall (24:7; 26:3; 50:24, untested). From the brief and chapter 22.
- **“Be fruitful, and multiply.”** Given to the creatures and to Adam and Eve (1:22, 28), renewed to Noah (9:1, 7), and carried into the covenant line (17:6; 28:3; 35:11; 48:4; the later references untested). From chapter 1.
- **God speaks in the plural; the council.** 1:26; 3:22; 11:7 (the last two untested). From chapter 1.
- **Image and likeness, from God to Adam to Seth.** 1:26–27; 5:1–3; 9:6 (untested). From chapter 1.
- **The LORD sees and provides.** 16:13–14; 21:17–19; 22:4, 8, 13–14; perhaps Peniel (32:30, untested). From chapter 22.
- **“Here am I.”** 22:1, 7, 11; to test at 27:1, 31:11, 46:2. From chapter 22.
- **The younger son chosen over the elder; Joseph as a deliverer.** From the brief; untested.
- **Abraham on the altar and Isaac on the altar.** [[Abr. 1:15–16]] (the angel of His presence frees Abraham; “Abraham, Abraham… my name is Jehovah”) beside [[Gen. 22:11]]. A connection no source makes, so it could only be a theme page's own (`STANDARDS.md` §8). From chapter 22.

## Reported by chapters 2–13 (not yet sorted)

- (2) Eden lost and restored, and the tree of life: Gen. 2:8–9; 3:22–24; Isa. 51:3; 1 Ne. 11:21–25; 15:22; Alma 42:2–5; Rev. 22:1–2 (may suit a Bible-wide page more than a Genesis one).
- (2) 'Bone of my bones' as covenant kinship: Gen. 2:23; 29:14; compare 2 Sam. 5:1.
- (2) Work and the ground (‘avad, adamah, shamar): Gen. 2:5, 7, 15; 3:17–19, 23–24; 4:2–12; 5:29 (beyond chapter 3 untested).
- (3) The way back into God's presence: hiding from the presence (3:8), driven out (3:23–24), cherubim keeping 'the way of the tree of life' (3:24); to test against 4:14, 16; 28:12–17; 32:30.
- (3) Clothing as status and covenant: 3:7, 21 (kuttonet); 27:15–16; 37:3, 23, 31–33 (Joseph's kuttonet); 41:42.
- (3) The cursed ground and toil ('itsavon): 3:16–17; 4:11–12; 5:29; 8:21.
- (3) The promised seed: 3:15 leading to the covenant seed of 12:7 and 22:17–18 (part of the Abrahamic covenant candidate).
- (4) Sacrifice as a similitude of Christ: 4:3–4 (Moses 5:5–8); 8:20; 22:2–13.
- (4) The younger brother chosen over the elder: Abel over Cain (4:4–5), Seth 'instead of Abel' (4:25); then Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, Ephraim.
- (4) Brothers in conflict and reconciliation: Cain and Abel (4:2–11, 'brother' seven times); Jacob and Esau (27; 33); Joseph and his brothers (37; 45; 50).
- (4) The promised seed: 'another seed' (4:25) after the seed of the woman (3:15), on to Abraham's seed (12; 22:18).
- (4) Two lines, Cain's and Seth's: 4:17–26; 5; 6:1–4 with Moses 8:13–15.
- (5) Walking with God: Enoch (5:22, 24), Noah (6:9), 'walk before me' (17:1; 24:40; 48:15), with Moses 6:34, 39 and 7:69.
- (5) Rest and comfort through Noah, pointing to Christ: 5:29; 6:6; 8:4, 9, 21; with Moses 7:48–61.
- (5) The two lines, Cain's and Seth's: 4:17–24 set against chapter 5, then 6:1–4 (Moses 8:13–15).
- (6) Covenant from Enoch through Noah to Abraham: 6:18 (JST 'as I have sworn unto thy father, Enoch'); 9:8–17; Moses 7:50–52; 17:1 ('walk before me, and be thou perfect', set beside 6:9).
- (6) Creation undone and remade: 1:31 beside 6:12; 6:7's list from the Creation; the Flood read as a re-creation (chapters 6–9).
- (6) Walking with God: 5:22–24; 6:9; 17:1; 24:40; 48:15.
- (7) Creation undone and renewed: Gen. 1:2, 6–10 → 7:11, 14, 22 → 8:1–3, 17; 9:1–3.
- (7) The covenant carried through the waters: Moses 8:2; Gen. 6:18; 7:1, 16–17, 23; 9:9–17; Isa. 54:9–10.
- (7) Saved through water, the Flood as a figure of baptism: Gen. 7:17–19; 1 Pet. 3:20–21 (test against Ex. 14 before proposing).
- (8) God remembers: remembering as the moment God acts to save and keep covenant (Gen. 8:1; 9:15–16; 19:29; 30:22).
- (8) Rest and comfort in Noah's name, fulfilled through his seed (Gen. 5:29; 8:4, 9, 21; Moses 7:48, 61).
- (8) The Flood as the Creation undone and renewed (Gen. 1; 7:11; 8:1–2, 17; 9:1, 7).
- (8) Prayer that obtains a covenant: Enoch for Noah's seed (Moses 7:50–51), Noah at the altar (JST Gen. 9:4–6), the covenant given (Gen. 9:8–17).
- (9) The everlasting covenant carried through the Flood: Enoch's covenant confirmed to Noah and later given to Abraham (Gen. 6:18; 9:9–17 with JST 9:15, 21–25; Moses 7:50–53; Gen. 12; 15; 17).
- (9) Tokens of the covenant: the bow (Gen. 9:12–17) and circumcision (Gen. 17:11), both called 'ot.
- (9) A father's blessings and curses on his sons that shape the nations (Gen. 9:25–27; 27:27–40; 48–49).
- (10) 'All the families of the earth': the nations and families of Gen. 10:5, 20, 31, 32 become the families blessed in Abraham (12:3; 18:18; 22:18; 26:4; 28:14), which Paul reads as the gospel (Gal. 3:8).
- (10) The chosen line told last: Japheth, Ham, then Shem (10:2–31); Joktan before Peleg (10:25–29; 11:16); then Ishmael before Isaac (25:12–19) and Esau before Jacob (36–37). This fits the brief's 'younger son chosen' candidate.
- (10) Babel and Babylon: Nimrod's kingdom (10:10) and the tower (11:1–9) set against Abraham's call (12:1–3).
- (11) Making a name vs. receiving one: Gen. 11:4, 10 (Shem = 'name'); 12:2; 17:5, 15; 32:28; Abr. 2:10.
- (11) Scattering and gathering: Gen. 11:4, 8–9; 12:3; 28:14; Abr. 2:9–11; D&C 133:7.
- (11) The barren mothers of the covenant line: Gen. 11:30; 16:1; 18:11–14; 21:1–2; 25:21; 29:31; 30:22–23; Isa. 51:2; 54:1; Gal. 4:27–28.
- (11) Babel/Babylon and Zion: Gen. 11:1–9; 2 Kgs. 25; D&C 1:16; 133:7, 14.
- (12) The Abrahamic covenant from promise to oath: 12:1–3, 7; 13:14–17; 15; 17; 22:15–18.
- (12) The land promised but not possessed: 12:6–7; Acts 7:5; Heb. 11:9–16; Gen. 23.
- (12) Egypt and the patriarchs, leading to the Exodus: 12:10–20 (nega', Ex. 11:1); 46–50.
- (12) The wife-sister stories: 12:10–20; 20; 26:6–11 (explained at 12:13).
- (12) Altars marking the land: 12:7–8; 13:4, 18; 22:9; 26:25; 33:20; 35:1–7.
- (13) The land promise, from sight to inheritance: Gen. 12:7; 13:14–17; 15:18–21; 17:8; 23 (the only land owned); 28:13–14; 50:24; with Heb. 11:9–16 and Acts 7:5.
- (13) Altars marking the land: Gen. 12:7–8; 13:4, 18; 22:9; 26:25; 33:20; 35:1–7.
- (13) One everlasting covenant from Enoch and Noah to Abraham: JST Gen. 9:21–23; the JST footnote at Gen. 13:14; Gen. 17:7.
- (13) The numberless seed (dust, stars, sand): Gen. 13:16; 15:5; 22:17; 28:14; D&C 132:30–31.
