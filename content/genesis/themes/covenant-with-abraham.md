The promise the Lord makes Abraham runs through the rest of Genesis. One reading takes chapters 12, 15, 17 and 22 in order as promise, formal initiation, expansion and formal confirmation,[@hopkin-covenant] and the covenant is then renewed with Isaac and again with Jacob.[@bd-abraham-covenant] The land stays a promise. The one piece of it Abraham comes to hold is a grave, and Genesis ends with Joseph making Israel swear to carry his bones there.

The Church teaches that the covenant reaches every family through Christ. Among the promises to Abraham was that “Christ would come through his lineage,”[@bd-abraham-covenant] and “Jesus Christ is at the center of the Abrahamic covenant.”[@nelson-everlasting-covenant] Abraham's own record names the blessing promised to all families as “the blessings of the Gospel” ([[Abr. 2:11]]).

## The path

1. [[Gen. 12:1–3]]: the call and the promise, “in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.”
2. [[Gen. 12:7]]: at Shechem the land is added, “Unto thy seed will I give this land.”
3. [[Gen. 15:5–6]]: seed as the stars, and Abram “believed in the LORD; and he counted it to him for righteousness.”
4. [[Gen. 15:18]]: the covenant made, “the LORD made a covenant with Abram.”
5. [[Gen. 17:1–8]]: a new name and “an everlasting covenant,” with the land “for an everlasting possession.”
6. [[Gen. 17:11]]: circumcision given as “a token of the covenant betwixt me and you.”
7. [[Gen. 22:16–18]]: the oath after Moriah, “By myself have I sworn, saith the LORD.”
8. [[Gen. 26:3–4]]: the oath renewed to Isaac, “I will perform the oath which I sware unto Abraham thy father.”
9. [[Gen. 28:13–15]]: to Jacob at Beth-el, “in thee and in thy seed shall all the families of the earth be blessed.”
10. [[Gen. 35:11–12]]: to Jacob again, “the land which I gave Abraham and Isaac, to thee I will give it.”
11. [[Gen. 23:4]], [[Gen. 23:20]]: the family's one holding in the land is a grave, “give me a possession of a buryingplace.”
12. [[Gen. 50:24–25]]: Joseph's last words, “God will surely visit you… ye shall carry up my bones from hence.”
13. [[Gal. 3:16]]: Paul reads the promise's word “seed” as one, “And to thy seed, which is Christ.”
