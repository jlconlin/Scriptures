Genesis begins this theme with a man and a woman who “hid themselves from the presence of the LORD God” ([[Gen. 3:8]]) and a brother who “went out from the presence of the LORD” ([[Gen. 4:16]]). It goes on to tell of God appearing, of a ladder and “the gate of heaven,” and of Jacob’s words, “I have seen God face to face” ([[Gen. 32:30]]). Restoration scripture says what was lost in Eden and through whom it is regained; John sets the ladder’s angels upon the Son of man.

## Hid, and driven out

Adam answers God, “I heard thy voice in the garden, and I was afraid, because I was naked; and I hid myself” ([[Gen. 3:10]]). God then places “Cherubims, and a flaming sword… to keep the way of the tree of life” ([[Gen. 3:24]]). Restoration scripture names the loss: Adam and Eve “saw him not; for they were shut out from his presence” ([[Moses 5:4]]), “cut off both temporally and spiritually from the presence of the Lord” ([[Alma 42:7]]).[@lds-scriptures] See [Genesis 3](/genesis/3/).

## “From thy face shall I be hid”

Cain tells the LORD, “from thy face shall I be hid” ([[Gen. 4:14]]); then “Cain went out from the presence of the LORD” ([[Gen. 4:16]]).[@lds-scriptures] Here the hiding is the loss Cain names, and the text tells of no return. See [Genesis 4](/genesis/4/) and [Brothers at Odds, and Reconciled](/genesis/themes/brothers-reconciled/).

## The LORD appeared

“The LORD appeared unto Abram” ([[Gen. 12:7]]), and again: “I am the Almighty God; walk before me, and be thou perfect” ([[Gen. 17:1]]). In Genesis’s own telling, “appeared” with God as subject is first used at 12:7; chapters 1–11 do not use it (Restoration scripture tells of earlier appearings: [[D&C 107:54]]).[@oshb-wlc][@lds-scriptures] Before Abram, Enoch and Noah “walked with God” ([[Gen. 5:24]]; [[Gen. 6:9]]).

## The gate of heaven

Jacob dreams of “a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven”; “the LORD stood above it” ([[Gen. 28:12–13]]). He wakes: “Surely the LORD is in this place; and I knew it not… this is none other but the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven” ([[Gen. 28:16–17]]). President Marion G. Romney likened temples to what Beth-el was to Jacob.[@romney-temples-1971] See the notes on [Genesis 28](/genesis/28/).

## Face to face

After the night at the Jabbok, Jacob names the place Peniel: “I have seen God face to face, and my life is preserved” ([[Gen. 32:30]]). To Esau he says, “I have seen thy face, as though I had seen the face of God” ([[Gen. 33:10]]). The Church’s study helps leave open who the “man” was: an angel, a mortal messenger, or God Himself.[@scripture-helps-gen-24-33] President Joseph Fielding Smith thought him more likely a messenger, and took Jacob’s “I have seen God” as not referring to the wrestling.[@ot-manual-gen-24-36] See the note on [Genesis 32:24](/genesis/32/).

Set side by side, as this page’s own comparison: the “presence” of 3:8 and 4:16 and the “face” of 4:14, 32:30 and 33:10 are one Hebrew noun, *panim*. A man and a woman hide from the face; Cain is hidden from it; Jacob says he has seen it and lived.[@lds-scriptures][@oshb-wlc] It is the ordinary word for “face” and “before” (141 times in Genesis), used also of fleeing a human face ([[Gen. 16:8]]; [[Gen. 35:1]]), so the comparison rests on what the verses say, not on the word.[@oshb-wlc]

## Before whom my fathers did walk

Near the end, Jacob blesses Joseph: “God, before whom my fathers Abraham and Isaac did walk, the God which fed me all my life long unto this day, the Angel which redeemed me from all evil, bless the lads” ([[Gen. 48:15–16]]).[@lds-scriptures] “Did walk” is the verb form of “walked with God” ([[Gen. 5:22, 24]]; [[Gen. 6:9]]), “walk before me” (17:1) and Abraham’s “The LORD, before whom I walk” ([[Gen. 24:40]]). Genesis has the form eight times; the other two are walking “through the land” ([[Gen. 13:17]]) and “walking in the garden” (3:8).[@oshb-wlc] Who the Angel is, Genesis does not say; see the note on [Genesis 48:16](/genesis/48/).

## The way back

Restoration scripture says the loss is undone through Christ. To the brother of Jared the Lord says, “ye are redeemed from the fall; therefore ye are brought back into my presence” ([[Ether 3:13]]); “the resurrection of Christ redeemeth mankind… and bringeth them back into the presence of the Lord” ([[Hel. 14:17]]).[@lds-scriptures]

Jesus tells Nathanael, “ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man” ([[John 1:51]]), an allusion to “the angels of God ascending and descending on it” ([[Gen. 28:12]]).[@frederick-use-of-ot] On one reading the angels now ascend and descend upon Jesus, so that He is “the true ‘gate of heaven.’”[@frederick-use-of-ot] See the note on [Genesis 28:12](/genesis/28/).

Genesis moves from hiding to seeing; scripture says the way back into God’s presence is through Jesus Christ.
