# Genesis: what the Church's own sources say on contested questions

Research notes for the Genesis brief. Every page below was fetched with `curl` (browser User-Agent) on 2026-10-03 from churchofjesuschrist.org, returned HTTP 200, and showed the heading given. Quotations are verbatim from the fetched page, each 40 words or fewer. Nothing here is interpretation; where a note says "flag", it only points at two quotations that sit differently.

Short names used below (URL prefix `https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/`):

- **BD** = Bible Dictionary, `scriptures/bd/<entry>?lang=eng`. No date on the entry pages (footer: "© 2013, 2025 by Intellectual Reserve, Inc."). Its own introduction (`scriptures/bd/introduction?lang=eng`, heading "Bible Dictionary") says:
  > “This dictionary is provided to help your study of the scriptures and is not intended as an official statement of Church doctrine or an endorsement of the historical and cultural views set forth.”
- **GS** = Guide to the Scriptures, `scriptures/gs/<entry>?lang=eng`. No date on the pages.
- **SM** = *Old Testament Student Manual: Genesis–2 Samuel* (Church Educational System; "Third edition © 1980, 1981, 2003"), `manual/old-testament-student-manual-genesis-2-samuel/<chapter>?lang=eng`.
- **SH** = *Scripture Helps: Old Testament* (2025), `manual/scripture-helps-old-testament/<chapter>?lang=eng`. Not on the list I was given; found through the links in the 2026 *Come, Follow Me* lessons. It is the Church's newest treatment of most of these questions.
- **CFM 2026** = *Come, Follow Me—For Home and Church: Old Testament 2026*, `manual/come-follow-me-for-home-and-church-old-testament-2026/<nn>?lang=eng`.

## 2. Days of creation; age of the earth; creation from existing matter; the two accounts and "spiritual creation"

**SM "Genesis 1–2: The Creation"**, (2-3) "How Old Is the Earth?" (1980) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/old-testament-student-manual-genesis-2-samuel/genesis-1-2-the-creation?lang=eng
> “While it is interesting to note these various theories, officially the Church has not taken a stand on the age of the earth.”
> “an attempt to establish any theory as the official position of the Church is not justifiable.”

(The section sets out three theories of the word *day*: 24 hours, a thousand years, "a period of an undetermined length of time".)

**SH "Genesis 1–2; Moses 2–3; Abraham 4–5"**, "How can I approach my study of the Creation?" and "How long did the Creation take?" (2025) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/scripture-helps-old-testament/02-genesis-1-2-moses-2-3-abraham-4-5?lang=eng
> “the scriptures do not provide details about how or when the earth was created.”
> “Using our measurement of time, we do not know how long it took God to create the earth.”

**Russell M. Nelson (Quorum of the Twelve), "The Creation," general conference, April 2000** — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2000/04/the-creation?lang=eng
> “Whether termed a day, a time, or an age, each phase was a period between two identifiable events—a division of eternity.”

**Creation from existing matter.** Topics and Questions, "Creation" (2023) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics/creation?lang=eng
> “in the work of the Creation, the Lord organized elements that had already existed. He did not create the world “out of nothing,” as some people believe.”

Also SM (2-5), same URL as (2-3), quoting Joseph Smith (*Teachings*, 350–52):
> “Now, the word create came from the word baurau which does not mean to create out of nothing; it means to organize; the same as a man would organize materials and build a ship.”

**Spiritual creation.** Moses 3:5 — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/pgp/moses/3?lang=eng
> “For I, the Lord God, created all things, of which I have spoken, spiritually, before they were naturally upon the face of the earth.”

GS "Spirit Creation" — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/gs/spirit-creation?lang=eng
> “The Lord created all things spiritually before He created them physically (Moses 3:5).”

SM (2-15) "Genesis 2:5. Is the Genesis Account a Record of the Spiritual Creation?", same URL as (2-3); the whole section is a quotation from Joseph Fielding Smith, *Doctrines of Salvation*, 1:76–77:
> “The account in Genesis, chapters one and two, is the account of the creation of the physical earth.”
> “It was, therefore, a spiritual creation and so remained until the fall when it became temporal, or mortal.”

**Genesis 1 and Genesis 2 as two accounts:** none found beyond the above. Searched SM chapter 2, SH Genesis 1–2 chapter, CFM 2026 lesson 03, GS "Create, Creation".

## 3. Evolution and the origin of Adam and Eve; death before the Fall

**"What does the Church believe about evolution?"**, *New Era*, Oct. 2016, 41 (unsigned) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/new-era/2016/10/to-the-point/what-does-the-church-believe-about-evolution?lang=eng
> “The Church has no official position on the theory of evolution. Organic evolution, or changes to species’ inherited traits over time, is a matter for scientific study. Nothing has been revealed concerning evolution.”
> “We are all descendants of Adam and Eve, our first parents, who were created in God’s image. There were no spirit children of Heavenly Father on the earth before Adam and Eve were created.”

(SH Genesis 1–2 chapter, "What is the Church’s position on evolution?" (2025), repeats this wording and quotes the Holland passage below.)

**"The Origin of Man"**, First Presidency (Joseph F. Smith, John R. Winder, Anthon H. Lund), Nov. 1909; reprinted *Ensign*, Feb. 2002, 26 — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/ensign/2002/02/the-origin-of-man?lang=eng
> “The word of the Lord declared that Adam was “the first man of all men” (Moses 1:34), and we are therefore in duty bound to regard him as the primal parent of our race.”

The *Ensign*'s 2002 introduction says the statement
> “expresses the Church’s doctrinal position on these matters.”

**Church History Topics, "Organic Evolution" (2022)** — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/history/topics/organic-evolution?lang=eng — reporting the First Presidency's 1931 request to Church leaders:
> “leave Geology, Biology, Archaeology and Anthropology, no one of which has to do with the salvation of the souls of mankind, to scientific research, while we magnify our calling in the realm of the Church.”

**Death before the Fall.** BD "Death" — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/death?lang=eng
> “Latter-day revelation teaches that there was no death on this earth before the Fall of Adam. Indeed, death entered the world as a direct result of the Fall (2 Ne. 2:22; Moses 6:48).”

BD "Fall of Adam and Eve" — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bd/fall-of-adam-and-eve?lang=eng
> “Before the Fall, there were no sin, no death, and no children.”
> “Adam became the “first flesh” upon the earth (Moses 3:7), meaning that he and Eve were the first to become mortal. After Adam fell, the whole creation fell and became mortal.”

Jeffrey R. Holland (Quorum of the Twelve), "Where Justice, Love, and Mercy Meet," general conference, April 2015 — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2015/04/where-justice-love-and-mercy-meet?lang=eng
> “there was an actual Adam and Eve who fell from an actual Eden, with all the consequences that fall carried with it.”
> “I do not know the details of what happened on this planet before that, but I do know these two were created under the divine hand of God”

(The same sentence continues that they lived "in a paradisiacal setting where there was neither human death nor future family".)

**Flag.** The 1980 SM chapter "Genesis 1–2", Points to Ponder (2-18), prints statements against evolution, among them this from Joseph Fielding Smith, *Doctrines of Salvation*, 1:141–42 (compare the 2016 and 2025 "no official position" wording above):
> “you cannot believe in this theory of the origin of man, and at the same time accept the plan of salvation as set forth by the Lord our God.”

## 8. The mark and curse of Cain (Gen. 4); the curse of Canaan (Gen. 9:20–27)

### What the Church now says and disavows

**Gospel Topics Essay "Race and the Priesthood"** (page citation: Gospel Topics Essays, 2016) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/gospel-topics-essays/race-and-the-priesthood?lang=eng
> “Today, the Church disavows the theories advanced in the past that black skin is a sign of divine disfavor or curse, or that it reflects unrighteous actions in a premortal life”
> “Over time, Church leaders and members advanced many theories to explain the priesthood and temple restrictions. None of these explanations is accepted today as the official doctrine of the Church.”

The essay names both passages as sources of those theories:
> “Those who accepted this view believed that God’s “curse” on Cain was the mark of a dark skin.”
> “Black servitude was sometimes viewed as a second curse placed upon Noah’s grandson Canaan as a result of Ham’s indiscretion toward his father.”

**SH "Genesis 3–4; Moses 4–5"**, "What do we know about the curse and mark placed upon Cain?" (2025) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/scripture-helps-old-testament/03-genesis-3-4-moses-4-5?lang=eng
> “Cain’s curse was that the ground would not grow crops for him, he would wander as a fugitive, and he would be separated from the presence of the Lord.”
> “We should avoid speculating about the nature or appearance of the mark placed on Cain or that the curse applied to anyone other than him. The Church rejects and condemns racial and cultural prejudice in any form.”

The same section also says:
> “It is unclear what Cain’s mark was, though many have speculated about it.”

**SH "Genesis 6–11; Moses 8"**, "Why did Noah curse Canaan?" (2025) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/scripture-helps-old-testament/06-moses-6-11-moses-8?lang=eng
> “The scriptures don’t clearly explain why Ham’s son Canaan was cursed for his father’s offense against Noah.”
> “Some have incorrectly used the cursing of Canaan to justify slavery and discrimination—particularly toward people of Black African descent.”

On the Joseph Smith Translation of Genesis 9, the same section says:
> “Joseph Smith’s inspired translation of the Bible adds that “a veil of darkness” would cover Canaan “that he shall be known among all men.” The meaning of this “veil of darkness” is unclear.”

**SH "Genesis 12–17; Abraham 1–2"**, "What were the curses mentioned by Abraham?" (2025; on Abraham 1:21–27) — https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/scripture-helps-old-testament/07-genesis-12-17-abraham-1-2?lang=eng
> “In the past, some have incorrectly associated the curse on Ham’s posterity with the priesthood and temple restriction pertaining to people of Black African descent in our dispensation. Today, the Church disavows this and other past theories”

**SH "Moses 7"**, endnote on Moses 7:8 (URL under question 7):
> “This “blackness” was not part of the Lord’s curse, which was directed at the land itself.”
