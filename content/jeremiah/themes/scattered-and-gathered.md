Jeremiah announces that Judah will be scattered among the nations, and in the same book the Lord promises to gather Israel again “out of all countries whither I have driven them” ([[Jer. 23:3]]). The gathering comes one by one, brings Israel and Judah back together, and will be remembered above the Exodus. The Church's edition places the gathering of chapter 3 “in the last days,”[@lds-scriptures] and the Bible Dictionary says of it: “This event of the latter days will supersede in proportion even the bringing of Israel out of Egypt by Moses.”[@bd-jeremiah]

The Church teaches that this gathering is under way. The Doctrine and Covenants records Moses committing its keys ([[D&C 110:11]]), and President Nelson saw Jeremiah's fishers and hunters in the missionaries who “have searched for those of scattered Israel.”[@nelson-gathering]

## The path

1. [[Jer. 9:16]]: the scattering announced: “I will scatter them also among the heathen.”
2. [[Jer. 3:14]]: one by one: “one of a city, and two of a family.”
3. [[Jer. 3:18]]: Judah and Israel return as one: “they shall come together out of the land of the north.”
4. [[Jer. 16:14–16]]: a deliverance to outshine the Exodus, carried out by “many fishers” and “many hunters.”
5. [[Jer. 23:3]]: the Lord gathers the flock His shepherds scattered: “I will gather the remnant of my flock.”
6. [[Jer. 23:7–8]]: the Exodus promise again, ending “they shall dwell in their own land.”
7. [[Jer. 29:14]]: to the exiles, a gathering wider than Babylon: “from all the nations.”
8. [[Jer. 30:3]]: one promise for both houses: “I will bring again the captivity of my people Israel and Judah.”
9. [[Jer. 31:9–10]]: the One who scattered is the One who gathers: “He that scattered Israel will gather him, and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.”
10. [[Jer. 32:37]]: during the siege, the promise stands: “I will gather them out of all countries.”
11. [[Jer. 50:4–5]]: Israel and Judah come together, joined to the Lord “in a perpetual covenant.”
12. [[D&C 110:11]]: Moses commits “the keys of the gathering of Israel from the four parts of the earth.”
