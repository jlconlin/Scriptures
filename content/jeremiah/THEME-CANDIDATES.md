# Jeremiah: theme candidates

Topics that run across several chapters, collected for possible theme pages (`content/jeremiah/themes/`; the format and what earns a page are in `STANDARDS.md` §8). Writers report them; the coordinator adds a line, or adds a chapter to an existing line. Most will stay chapter notes. None is chosen until the author chooses.

This file lives outside `themes/` because the build publishes every Markdown file there as a page.

- Shuv, turning back: the sin (mešubah, “backsliding”) and the cure (“return”) share one root: 3:1, 6–8, 10–12, 14, 19, 22 (ch. 3)
- Gathering from the north, “one of a city, and two of a family”, and Judah and Israel reunited: 3:12–18; Jer. 16:14–21; 23:3–8 (ch. 3)

- Israel and Judah as two sisters, Judah the worse: 3:6–11, 18; Ezek. 16, 23 (ch. 3)
- Shepherds “according to mine heart” against the false shepherds: 3:15; 23:1–4 (ch. 3)
- The heart (circumcision, washing, the heart reached and pained): Jer. 4:4, 9, 14, 18, 19; Deut. 10:16; 30:6; Mosiah 5:2, 7 (ch. 4)
- False promises of peace: Jer. 4:10; 6:14; 14:13–15; 23:17 (ch. 4)

- Creation undone: Jer. 4:23–26 (Gen. 1:2; Moses 2:2) (ch. 4)
- "Not a full end", the limit on judgment: Jer. 4:27; 5:10, 18; Lev. 26:44; 1 Ne. 10:3 (ch. 4)
- Zion as a woman, from travail to Lamentations: Jer. 4:30–31; Lam. 1:17, 20 (ch. 4)
- Prosperity breeding apostasy: Jer. 5:7, 5:27–28 (Deut. 32:15) (ch. 5)
- False prophets and false security in Jerusalem: Jer. 5:12–13, 5:31 (and 14:13, 28) (ch. 5)
- The remnant, 'not a full end': Jer. 4:27, 5:10, 5:18 (ch. 5)
- The search for one righteous person: Jer. 5:1 (Gen. 18; Ezek. 22:30; Isa. 59:16) (ch. 5)
- Jerusalem ripe for destruction, with Nephi: Jer. 5:9, 29 (1 Ne. 17:35, 43) (ch. 5)
- Refusing the warning: “We will not walk therein… We will not hearken” (6:16–17), the uncircumcised ear (6:10); compare 7:13, 25:4 (ch. 6)
- “Peace, peace” and the false prophets: 6:13–15; 8:10–12; 14:13; 23:17 (with Lehi's Jerusalem, KnoWhy 451) (ch. 6)

- Obedience over sacrifice: 6:19–20; 7:21–23 (ch. 6)

- The queen of heaven and household idolatry: Jer. 7:18; 44:15–19 (ch. 7)

- The Lord forbids intercession (“pray not for this people”): Jer. 7:16; 11:14; 14:11; 15:1 (ch. 7)
- “I will be your God, and ye shall be my people”: Jer. 7:23; 11:4; 31:33 (ch. 7)

- Israel as the Lord's olive tree: Jer. 11:16–17; Jacob 5 (ch. 11)
- The Lord who tries the reins and the heart: Jer. 11:20; 17:10; 20:12 (ch. 11)
