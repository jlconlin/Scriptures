# Jeremiah: open questions for review

Only what the author has to decide. Delete an item once it is resolved. (The conventions applied while writing, and the reader questions no source answered, are in `BRIEF.md`.)

- **1:5.** After the Latter-day Saint reading (premortal existence, foreordination), the note briefly gives the reading that takes the verse as God's decree only. Keep it, or leave it out?
  **Keep it**
- **13:4.** The linen girdle: Keil's literal journey to the Euphrates is set beside the manual's “parable.” Give only the manual's reading?
- **16:2.** The marriage ban is literal; the manual's figurative reading (“may not have been a literal one”) is kept as a possibility. Keep it?
- **23:5–6.** The Branch is read as Christ in the Millennium (McConkie through the manual). Add the first coming too?
- **25:11.** The seventy years are counted two ways, from Church sources. Mention the symbolic reading (Keil) at all?
- **35:7.** A paragraph on the later *History of the Rechabites* (KnoWhy #710) is the only sourced answer to what became of the promise in verse 19, but it is reception history. Keep or cut?
- **38:6.** Which imprisonment Nephi meant (1 Ne. 7:14): the note gives three proposals without choosing. Lean toward one?
- **19 (christ).** The jar broken in the valley of graves set beside Matthew's potter's field: the manual doesn't make that link. Keep or cut?
- **45:4.** “This whole land”: Keil reads “the whole earth”; the plain words say “land.” Which?
- **Theme pages.** Three were written on 2026-10-02 and are not pushed: The Shepherds and the Branch; Scattered and Gathered; The Covenant Written in the Heart. Keep, change, or drop each? (Why the cup and “root out and plant” were not written is in `THEME-CANDIDATES.md`.)
- **Theme pages, navigation.** Jeremiah has no guides, so the header has no link to its theme pages; they are reached at `/jeremiah/themes/` and from chapter notes. Add a header link for a book with themes but no guides? (A change to the site's look, so it waits for you.)
- **25:15 and the `christ` section of 25; Isaiah 51 “The cup taken away.”** These say the cup the nations drink is the cup the Savior drank. The theme research found no Church source that links Jeremiah's or Isaiah's cup to Gethsemane (the footnotes run the other way; one BYU article makes the link). Keep the link, soften it to “the same image,” or cut it?
- **23:7.** The note says the promise is repeated “in nearly the same words” in 16:14–15. In Hebrew 16:14 and 23:7 match except the verb, and 16:15 and 23:8 are reworded (the theme page says so). Tighten the note to match?
- **New covenant page, last section.** It follows the manuals and Joseph Smith's 1833 letter: the covenant was offered by Christ, rejected then, and is offered again in the Restoration. The Saxton letter is quoted for that. Is this the emphasis you want, or should the page stop at Hebrews and the Last Supper?
