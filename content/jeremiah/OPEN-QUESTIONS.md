# Jeremiah: open questions for review

Judgment calls waiting for the author. Whoever writes or checks a chapter reports its items, and the coordinator adds them here instead of deciding them silently. Only items that need the author's judgment belong here; anything the standard already decides is just done. Delete an item once it is resolved.

## The brief

The brief was drafted on 2026-10-02 without the author. These are the choices in it that are the author's to confirm or change.

- **Divisions.** Six, following the Bible Dictionary's outline by reign (1–6, 7–20, 21–29, 30–33, 34–45, 46–52). Their names and blurbs are in `book.yaml`.
- **Dates.** The chapters use the Bible Dictionary's dates (Jerusalem falls in 586 B.C.).
- **Chapters 50–52.** The Bible Dictionary says chapters 50–51 “in their present form are later than Jeremiah.” The chapters report this once, citing it, and go no further. Should they say more, or less?
- **Lehi and Jeremiah.** The brief treats this as the book's main Restoration connection but tells writers not to force it into every chapter. A guide page on Jeremiah and Lehi's Jerusalem may be worth writing.
- **No focus questions.** The chapters were written from a careful reader's questions, not the author's. Chapters the author has questions about can be revised around them.
- **Going live.** `book.yaml` has `status: preview`. Remove that line to publish the book; the home page's “More of the Old Testament” card (`COMING_SOMEDAY` in `src/site.mjs`) still names Jeremiah and should change then.
