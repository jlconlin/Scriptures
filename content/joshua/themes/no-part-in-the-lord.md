In Joshua a “part” (Hebrew *ḥēleq*) is first a share of ground, and one tribe has none: its inheritance is the LORD. Late in the book two and a half tribes with land of their own fear that their children will be told “Ye have no part in the LORD.” Read together, these passages carry the word from acres to the LORD Himself.

## A part of the ground

*Ḥēleq* is in Joshua nine times, in nine verses.[@oshb-wlc] Three are the “seven parts” of the survey. Others are a share of ground: Caleb “a part among the children of Judah” ([[Josh. 15:13]]), and Simeon inside Judah, because “the part of the children of Judah was too much for them” ([[Josh. 19:9]]).

## No part, because the LORD is their inheritance

The list of allotments stops twice for Levi: “the sacrifices of the LORD God of Israel made by fire are their inheritance, as he said unto them” ([[Josh. 13:14]]); “the LORD God of Israel was their inheritance, as he said unto them” ([[Josh. 13:33]]). “As he said” looks back to [[Num. 18:20]], “I am thy part and thine inheritance.”[@lds-scriptures] Joshua says it a third way at Shiloh: “the Levites have no part among you; for the priesthood of the LORD is their inheritance” ([[Josh. 18:7]]). They “had no inheritance in Canaan,” but had “the tithe,” “48 cities,” and “a claim on the alms of the people.”[@bd-levites]

## Out of their inheritance

The Levite heads came “not to Joshua, the military commander, but rather to Eleazar, the priest,”[@ot-minute-josh-21] and “the children of Israel gave unto the Levites out of their inheritance, at the commandment of the LORD” ([[Josh. 21:3]]). Those with ground gave from it to the tribe with none.

## “Ye have no part in the LORD”

The eastern tribes answer that they built the altar “for fear of this thing”: “your children might speak unto our children… What have ye to do with the LORD God of Israel?… ye have no part in the LORD” ([[Josh. 22:24–25]]). It is a fear, not a verdict; Joshua never says it of any tribe. One older reading: “He does not concern you; He is our God.”[@keil-joshua] In 22:25, 27 the sense is “share or interest in,” listed apart from the share of land, which Levi was denied.[@bdb]

Set beside each other, 18:7 and 22:25 share two words, *ʾên ḥēleq*, “no part.”[@oshb-wlc] In 18:7 the tribe with no part among Israel has the LORD; in 22:25 tribes that have ground fear having no part in the LORD.[@lds-scriptures]

They point to an altar “not for burnt offering, nor for sacrifice; but that it may be a witness,” so that “we might do the service of the LORD” at “the altar of the LORD our God that is before his tabernacle” ([[Josh. 22:26–29]]).

Phinehas answers: “This day we perceive that the LORD is among us” ([[Josh. 22:31]]). The altar is named “a witness between us that the LORD is God” ([[Josh. 22:34]]).

<h2 id="christ">The inheritance that is the Lord Himself</h2>

The LORD who was Levi's inheritance ([[Josh. 13:33]]) is Jehovah, “the premortal Jesus Christ.”[@bd-jehovah] Psalm 16 uses the same noun: “The LORD is the portion of mine inheritance and of my cup” ([[Ps. 16:5]]).[@oshb-wlc] Psalm 16 is “A messianic psalm of David,”[@lds-scriptures] and Peter quotes its verses 8–11 of Christ's resurrection: “his soul was not left in hell, neither his flesh did see corruption” ([[Acts 2:25–31]]).[@lds-scriptures] Verse 5 is not among the verses he quotes.

Christ “is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death… they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance” ([[Heb. 9:15]]); the “inheritance incorruptible” comes “by the resurrection of Jesus Christ from the dead” ([[1 Pet. 1:3–4]]).

Set beside [[Josh. 18:7]] are the Lord's words in 1832: “all they who receive this priesthood receive me… and he that receiveth my Father receiveth my Father’s kingdom; therefore all that my Father hath shall be given unto him” ([[D&C 84:35–38]]). Joshua gives the priesthood as the inheritance of the tribe with no ground; the revelation ties receiving the priesthood to receiving “all that my Father hath.” The section names “the sons of Moses and of Aaron” ([[D&C 84:34]]).[@lds-scriptures] See also [My Portion for Ever](/psalms/themes/my-portion-for-ever/).

## “The LORD is my portion”

“The LORD is my portion, saith my soul; therefore will I hope in him” ([[Lam. 3:24]]). “The blessings of the oath and covenant of the priesthood are not just for priesthood holders. God’s greatest blessings come to men and women who are sealed to each other in the temple.”[@dc-student-manual-2017-84] President Nelson: “no earthly reward—no other success—could compensate for the bounties the Lord will bestow upon those who love Him, keep His commandments… and endure to the end.”[@dc-student-manual-2017-84]
