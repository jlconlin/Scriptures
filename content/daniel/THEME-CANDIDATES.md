# Daniel: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). None is chosen: the author selects them after the chapters are written, and no page is written until the author says so. With twelve chapters, a topic that lives in one chapter belongs in that chapter's notes.

## Noticed while setting up (2026-10-08; not yet tested against the chapters)

- **The kingdom that shall never be destroyed** (2:44; 4:3, 34; 6:26; 7:14, 18, 27): said by Daniel, by Nebuchadnezzar, by Darius, and in the vision, in nearly the same words each time. D&C 65.
- **“There is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets”** (1:17; 2:19–30, 47; 4:8–9; 5:11–16; 8:15–16; 9:21–23; 10:12–14): wisdom given, never Daniel's own; Joseph in Genesis beside him.
- **The most High ruleth in the kingdom of men** (2:21; 4:17, 25, 32; 5:18–23): kings set up and removed.
- **Delivered, “but if not”** (1; 3; 6; 12:1): faithfulness in a foreign court, with and without rescue.
