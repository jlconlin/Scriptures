# Daniel: theme candidates

Topics that run across the book and might become theme pages (`STANDARDS.md` §8). None is chosen: the author selects them after the chapters are written, and no page is written until the author says so. With twelve chapters, a topic that lives in one chapter belongs in that chapter's notes.

## Noticed while setting up (2026-10-08; not yet tested against the chapters)

- **The kingdom that shall never be destroyed** (2:44; 4:3, 34; 6:26; 7:14, 18, 27): said by Daniel, by Nebuchadnezzar, by Darius, and in the vision, in nearly the same words each time. D&C 65.
- **“There is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets”** (1:17; 2:19–30, 47; 4:8–9; 5:11–16; 8:15–16; 9:21–23; 10:12–14): wisdom given, never Daniel's own; Joseph in Genesis beside him.
- **The most High ruleth in the kingdom of men** (2:21; 4:17, 25, 32; 5:18–23): kings set up and removed.
- **Delivered, “but if not”** (1; 3; 6; 12:1): faithfulness in a foreign court, with and without rescue.

## Reported by chapters 1–12 (not yet sorted)

- (1) God gave: wisdom and outcomes given, never Daniel's own (1:2, 1:9, 1:17; then 2:19-30).
- (1) The temple vessels taken, profaned and returned (1:2; 5:2-4, 23; Ezra 1:7).
- (1) Daniel and Joseph: renamed, interpreters of dreams, set against the same 'magicians' (1:7, 1:17, 1:20; 2:27-30; Gen. 41).
- (1) Faithful in a foreign court by request, not defiance (1:8-13; 3:16-18; 6:10).
- (2) The revealer of secrets, and wisdom that is not the interpreter's own (2:19–30, 47), with Joseph (Gen. 41:16).
- (2) He removes kings and sets up kings (2:21, 37–38, 44; then 4:17), with Jer. 27:5–6.
- (2) The kingdom that shall never be destroyed (2:44; 7:14, 18, 27), with D&C 65 and 138:44.
- (2) What a king's confession amounts to (2:47; then 3:28–29; 4:8, 34–37).
- (2) The Aramaic section opens and closes with the four kingdoms (chapters 2 and 7).
- (3) Delivered, "but if not": 3:15–18, 28–29 (the verb shezib four times here; chapter 6 by the writer's count: 6:14, 16, 20, 27).
- (3) What the kings confess: 2:47; 3:28–29; 4:34–37; 6:26–27. Draper traces the steps from 2:47 to 3:28–29 and on to chapter 4; the claim that each confession matches what the king has just seen is not sourced.
- (3) Malicious accusation of the faithful official: 3:8; 6:4–5, 24 (the same Aramaic idiom; the shared motive of rank is not sourced).
- (3) The angel sent: 3:28; 6:22.
- (4) The kings' confessions of Daniel's God: 2:47; 3:28-29; 4:1-3, 34-37; 6:26-27.

- (6) Daniel and Joseph: 6:3 with Gen. 41:38-41.
