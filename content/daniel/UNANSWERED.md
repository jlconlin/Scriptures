# Daniel: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief. `scripts/batch-notes.mjs` appends here.

*(`scripts/batch-notes.mjs` appends these, by chapter.)*
- **1:** How three years of training (1:5, 1:18) fit 'the second year of the reign of Nebuchadnezzar' (2:1). The writer read Keil's answer and left it for chapter 2; chapter 1 does not raise it; How old Daniel and his friends were when taken: the text gives no ages, and the only figure read (Keil's fifteen to eighteen) is a guess and was left out; Why the conqueror put the temple vessels in his own god's treasury (1:2): no source read says what the act meant.
- **2:** 2:1: the year B.C. of Nebuchadnezzar's second year (no source read states it); 2:2: what each of the four titles (magicians, astrologers, sorcerers, soothsayers) means; only 'Chaldeans' is explained; 2:4: why the book changes to Aramaic here (no Latter-day Saint source read gives a reason; the note says so); 2:39: in what sense the second kingdom is 'inferior' (only Keil answers); 2:45: which mountain the stone is cut from (only Keil's reading, Zion, given as his); 2:49: what 'sat in the gate of the king' means (paraphrased in the plain words only; no note).
- **3:** Where was the plain of Dura (3:1)? No source was read through for it; Why "seven times" hotter (3:19)? The only sources were the Student Manual relaying Clarke and one line of Keil; How tall is sixty cubits in modern measure (3:1)? The only figure read was in a talk as the Student Manual prints it; What became of the bonds (3:23–25)? The text does not say, and no source read says it of Daniel 3; Why were the three accused: was it resentment of their appointment (3:8, 12)? No source read states the motive; Why is Daniel absent (3:12)? Only Keil's admitted guesses were found; the note gives them as guesses.
- **4:** When in Nebuchadnezzar's reign the dream and the madness fall: no source read dates them; What the band of iron and brass (4:15) is: Daniel does not explain it, and the two readings reported are guesses; Whether the 'seven times' are years: the lexicon, Josephus and the Church's footnote at 4:34 say years, the footnote at 4:16 allows seasons, and the text does not say; A medical name for the king's condition: left out. Keil gives one, but the brief asks for the text's own description.
- **5:** 5:2, 11, 18: why Nebuchadnezzar is called Belshazzar's father. No source read settles it; the note says so; 5:8: why the wise men could not even read the writing. The text gives no reason; one older reading is reported; 5:10-14: why Belshazzar did not know Daniel, and what place Daniel held in this reign. The book does not say; 5:7, 29: what the scarlet robe and gold chain signified as honors. Only the Student Manual and Keil address it, and the writer left it out; the footnote's link to Joseph (Genesis 41:42) is no longer repeated in the chapter; 5:6, 12, 16: whether the repeated 'loosening knots' wording is a deliberate play on words. No source read says.
- **6:** Who was the angel of 6:22? The text does not say and no source read does; Which ruler known to history was Darius the Median, and how do his reign and Cyrus's relate (6:28)? Only the Bible Dictionary's 'impossible to identify' and Josephus's identification were read; Why did the king sign the decree (6:7-9)? The text gives no reason; only one older commentary's reconstruction was found; Why two seals on the stone (6:17) beyond the verse's own 'that the purpose might not be changed'? No source read explains it; How could Daniel claim innocence before the king when he broke the decree (6:22)? No source read addresses it; the note gives only his words.
- **7:** 7:12: why the other beasts' lives were 'prolonged for a season and time' after their dominion was taken away. No source read answers it; only the plain-words section mentions it; 7:15, 7:28: why Daniel ends troubled when the vision ends with the saints receiving the kingdom. No source read says; 7:5-7: what the three ribs, the four heads and the ten horns each stand for. The Student Manual gives only general senses (ferocity, governing power).
- **8:** Daniel 8:5: what it signifies that the goat 'touched not the ground'; no source read says; Daniel 8:24: whose power is behind the king whose power is 'not by his own power'; the text does not say and no source read does; Daniel 8:1: a year B.C. for the third year of Belshazzar; no source read gives one; Daniel 8:23: 'understanding dark sentences' has no note; the Church's footnote ('riddles') is beside the verse and nothing further was found.
- **9:** 9:26: who "the prince that shall come" is; the text does not name him and no source read does; 9:26: what "the end thereof shall be with a flood" means; no source read explains it; 9:27: what the "one week" and its "midst" are; the Student Manual says it has not been satisfactorily explained; 9:24: why seventy sevens; Gabriel does not explain the number, and the one argument found (sabbath years) is disputed by the source that reports it; 9:21: whether Daniel saw Gabriel with wings; not researched, and the note gives only what the Hebrew and one translation allow.
- **10:** Why Daniel did not return to Jerusalem after Cyrus's decree (10:1): the text gives no reason, and the only answer found was Keil's conjecture, which was not used; What the contest between the messenger and the prince of Persia was over (10:13): neither the text nor any source read says; What it means that Michael 'holdeth with' the messenger (10:21): the verb's root is the 'strengthen' of verses 18-19, but no lexicon page was read for this form's sense; What Uphaz and the beryl are (10:5-6): left unanswered on purpose as a curiosity.
- **11:** Who 'the prince of the covenant' is (11:22): only Keil treats it, reporting and rejecting the high priest Onias III; left in the paraphrase with no note; 'The desire of women' (11:37): Keil reports that it has been read of the love of women and of a goddess; no source settles it; Verses 40-45 in detail (Edom, Moab and Ammon escaping; tidings from the east and north; 'between the seas'): no Latter-day Saint source read explains them; Which god 'the God of forces' is (11:38): the lexicon gives no name and Keil reports only 'very great diversity of opinion'; The pairing of individual verses in 11:25-31 with Antiochus's Egyptian campaigns and the Roman intervention: Josephus tells the events but does not join them to these verses; only Keil does, and he is already at his limit of a third of the notes.
- **12:** 12:2: why Daniel is told 'many' shall awake when the Savior and D&C 29:26 say 'all'. Keil's explanation was read by the writer and left out; no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source read addresses it, so the note states the difference without resolving it; 12:5: who the 'other two' on the banks of the river are. No source read says; 12:8: why a prophet does not understand his own vision. No source read addresses it directly; 12:7, 11-12: what the three periods measure. The Student Manual says this has not been revealed, and the chapter says so.
