# Daniel: decided while writing

What reviewers and the coordinator settled chapter by chapter, by the standard and the book's brief, without the author. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief (author, 2026-10-11: “We don't have to record every decision of every verse”). Nothing is added here any more. Search it for a chapter when that chapter is edited; nobody needs to read it through.

Settled by the reviewers on 2026-10-08, by the standard and this brief, without the author; any of them can be overturned.
