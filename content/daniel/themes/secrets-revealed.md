In Daniel 1–6 God reveals secrets and Daniel interprets them. The pattern changes: kings dream and Daniel interprets (Dan. 2, 4, 5); in chapter 7 Daniel dreams and a bystander interprets.[@ludlow-son-of-man] From chapter 8 a messenger is told, “make this man to understand the vision” ([[Dan. 8:16]]). At the end Daniel is told to seal the book and says, “I understood not.” The same verb, “understanding” in [[Dan. 1:17]] and “understood” in [[Dan. 12:8]], stands at both ends.[@oshb-wlc] In John's vision a different sealed book is opened by the Lamb.

## “There is a God in heaven that revealeth secrets”

Daniel's gift comes from God: “God gave them knowledge and skill… and Daniel had understanding in all visions and dreams” ([[Dan. 1:17]]). He asks his companions to seek mercy “concerning this secret,” and “then was the secret revealed unto Daniel in a night vision” ([[Dan. 2:18–19]]). To the king he says, “this secret is not revealed to me for any wisdom that I have” ([[Dan. 2:30]]); beside it stands Gen. 41:16, Joseph's “It is not in me.”[@lds-scriptures] “Daniel, like Joseph,” is “careful to point out” that the wisdom is God's.[@bd-daniel-book] The king answers: “a revealer of secrets” ([[Dan. 2:47]]). See [Daniel 2](/daniel/2/); the dream itself is on [the kingdom page](/daniel/themes/kingdom-never-destroyed/).

## The interpreter at court

Nebuchadnezzar says, “the spirit of the holy gods is in thee, and no secret troubleth thee” ([[Dan. 4:9]]). The queen tells Belshazzar of “an excellent spirit, and knowledge, and understanding, interpreting of dreams, and shewing of hard sentences, and dissolving of doubts” found “in the same Daniel” ([[Dan. 5:12]]). Belshazzar says to Daniel, “I have even heard of thee, that the spirit of the gods is in thee, and that light and understanding and excellent wisdom is found in thee” ([[Dan. 5:14]]). See [Daniel 4](/daniel/4/) and [Daniel 5](/daniel/5/).

## Made to understand

In chapter 7 the dream is Daniel's own; before, “kings received the dreams and then Daniel interpreted them.” Now “one of them that stood by” gives the interpretation ([[Dan. 7:16]]), “presumably an angel.”[@ludlow-son-of-man] Daniel's angelic messengers are set beside the angel through whom God sent John the vision: “by his angel” ([[Rev. 1:1]]).[@sc-knowhy-492] Gabriel is told, “make this man to understand the vision” ([[Dan. 8:16]]); of the vision Daniel says, “none understood it” ([[Dan. 8:27]]). He then “understood by books” ([[Dan. 9:2]]), and Gabriel comes “to give thee skill and understanding” ([[Dan. 9:22]]). The messenger of chapter 10 says, “I am come to make thee understand” ([[Dan. 10:14]]). See [Daniel 7](/daniel/7/), [8](/daniel/8/), [9](/daniel/9/) and [10](/daniel/10/).

## “Seal the book”

Already at the end of chapter 8: “shut thou up the vision” ([[Dan. 8:26]]). At the end of the book: “shut up the words, and seal the book, even to the time of the end” ([[Dan. 12:4]]). Daniel says, “I heard, but I understood not,” and is told: “Go thy way, Daniel: for the words are closed up and sealed till the time of the end… none of the wicked shall understand; but the wise shall understand” ([[Dan. 12:8–10]]). The interpretation of the chapter's time periods “has not been revealed.”[@ot-manual-28] Much of the visions' meaning “was not revealed even to Daniel.”[@ot-seminary-daniel] “No prophet of the Restoration has opened” the sealed visions.[@draper-exile] Joseph Smith joined Daniel's vision of the Son of Man to “the wise shall understand.”[@tpjs-2007-ch-21] Daniel's words are listed among “other sealed books to come forth in the last days,” beside the sealed part of John's vision ([[Rev. 10:4]]; [[1 Ne. 14:25–26]]).[@tvedtnes-sealed-books] Dan. 12:9 is linked to 1 Ne. 14:26, 2 Ne. 27:10, Ether 4:5, D&C 35:18 and JS—H 1:65.[@lds-scriptures] These references do not identify Daniel's words as the sealed portion of the Book of Mormon. See [Daniel 12](/daniel/12/).

## A different sealed book, opened by the Lamb

Daniel's book is sealed; in John's vision a different sealed book appears: “a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals,” and the question is “Who is worthy to open the book?” ([[Rev. 5:1–2]]). The Lion of Juda “hath prevailed to open the book” ([[Rev. 5:5]]); the song is “Thou art worthy to take the book, and to open the seals thereof: for thou wast slain” ([[Rev. 5:9]]). That book “contains the revealed will, mysteries, and the works of God… concerning this earth during the seven thousand years” ([[D&C 77:6]]).[@lds-scriptures] Jesus Christ is “the only person worthy to open and read the sealed book.”[@nt-manual-54]
