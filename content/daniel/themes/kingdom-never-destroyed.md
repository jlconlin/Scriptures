In Daniel 2–5 God gives kingdoms and takes them away. Beside them stands a kingdom God sets up that “shall never be destroyed”; two kings say in their own decrees that God's kingdom lasts; and in chapter 7 dominion, described in the kings' words, is given to “one like the Son of man” and to the saints.

## Given and taken away

Daniel's hymn says it first: God “removeth kings, and setteth up kings” ([[Dan. 2:21]]; see the note on that verse).

Nebuchadnezzar boasts of Babylon and hears “The kingdom is departed from thee” ([[Dan. 4:30–31]]) “until thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men” ([[Dan. 4:32]]). Daniel tells Belshazzar, “thou… hast not humbled thine heart, though thou knewest all this” ([[Dan. 5:22]]); his kingdom is “numbered… and finished,” and “given to the Medes and Persians” ([[Dan. 5:26]], [[Dan. 5:28]]).

## What the kings say

Nebuchadnezzar writes to all nations that “his kingdom is an everlasting kingdom” ([[Dan. 4:1]], [[Dan. 4:3]]); restored, he blesses Him “whose dominion is an everlasting dominion” ([[Dan. 4:34]]). Darius decrees of the God of Daniel, “his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed” ([[Dan. 6:26]]); the Church's footnote joins that phrase to [[Dan. 2:44]].[@lds-scriptures] How far the kings' own belief went the text does not settle: it is hard to know whether Nebuchadnezzar stopped worshiping other gods,[@draper-exile] and his praise does not necessarily mean conversion.[@ot-manual-28] See the notes on [[Dan. 2:47]] and [[Dan. 4:37]].

## Given to the Son of man

The beasts “had their dominion taken away” ([[Dan. 7:12]]). Then one “like the Son of man” comes to the Ancient of days, and “there was given him dominion, and glory, and a kingdom” ([[Dan. 7:13–14]]). The saints “shall take the kingdom” ([[Dan. 7:18]]), and it “shall be given to the people of the saints of the most High” ([[Dan. 7:27]]). Verses 14 and 27 use passive verbs and do not name the giver.

**Connection made by this page:** the words the kings used of God's kingdom, set beside what is given to the Son of man.[@lds-scriptures] “Dominion is an everlasting dominion” in Nebuchadnezzar's mouth ([[Dan. 4:34]]) and of the Son of man ([[Dan. 7:14]]) are the same two Aramaic words, *sholtan* and *alam*; and “shall not be destroyed” at 2:44, 6:26 and 7:14 is one verb, *chabal*.[@oshb-wlc]

**Connection made by this page:** the verb used when kings and beasts lose their rule, set beside the Son of man's dominion, which “shall not pass away” ([[Dan. 7:14]]).[@lds-scriptures] It is one verb, *adah*: “removeth,” 2:21; “departed,” 4:31; “took,” 5:20; “taken away,” 7:12; “take away,” 7:26. Its other three verses in Daniel are 3:27, of the fire, and 6:8, 12, of the law that “altereth not.”[@oshb-wlc] The kings are not said to have foreseen this. For the image's and beasts' kingdoms see [[Dan. 2:38]], [[Dan. 7:17]].

## The Son of Man and the stone

“The ‘Son of Man’ is Jesus Christ,” and latter-day revelation names the Ancient of days as Adam.[@bd-daniel-book] Joseph Smith taught that Adam will there hand his stewardship over to Christ;[@jsp-discourse-1839-a-richards] see the note on [[Dan. 7:9]]. Before the high priest Jesus said, “I am: and ye shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven” ([[Mark 14:62]]). By the title He often signaled that He would fulfil Daniel's prophecy.[@scripture-helps-dan]

For the stone, the gospel is to “roll forth… as the stone which is cut out of the mountain without hands” ([[D&C 65:2]]).[@lds-scriptures] Daniel “foresaw and foretold the establishment of the kingdom of God in the latter days, never again to be destroyed” ([[D&C 138:44]]).[@lds-scriptures] President Kimball and Elder Christofferson have taught that The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints is that kingdom;[@kimball-stone][@christofferson-why-church] see the note on [[Dan. 2:44]]. D&C 65 asks that the kingdom go forth and looks to the days when “the Son of Man shall come down in heaven… to meet the kingdom of God which is set up on the earth” ([[D&C 65:5]]): “may the kingdom of God go forth, that the kingdom of heaven may come” ([[D&C 65:6]]).
