In Daniel every king learns that his kingdom is lent. God “removeth kings, and setteth up kings” ([[Dan. 2:21]]). Nebuchadnezzar loses his throne “until thou know that the most High ruleth in the kingdom of men” ([[Dan. 4:32]]), and Belshazzar's kingdom is numbered and finished in a night. Beside these the God of heaven sets up one kingdom “which shall never be destroyed” ([[Dan. 2:44]]). Two kings confess it in their decrees, and in chapter 7 it is given to “one like the Son of man” and to the saints ([[Dan. 7:13–14]], [[Dan. 7:27]]). Jesus took Daniel's words as His own before the high priest: “ye shall see the Son of man sitting on the right hand of power, and coming in the clouds of heaven” ([[Mark 14:62]]).

The Church teaches that this kingdom has begun. The Lord said that the gospel shall “roll forth unto the ends of the earth, as the stone which is cut out of the mountain without hands” ([[D&C 65:2]]), and Elder D. Todd Christofferson has taught that the Church is “that prophesied latter-day kingdom, not created by man but set up by the God of heaven.”[@christofferson-why-church]

## The path

1. [[Dan. 2:21]]: Daniel's hymn names the pattern before the dream is told: “he removeth kings, and setteth up kings.”
2. [[Dan. 2:44]]: the stone of the dream is a kingdom the God of heaven sets up, “which shall never be destroyed.”
3. [[Dan. 4:31–32]]: the boasting king hears “The kingdom is departed from thee” (see the note on [[Dan. 4:30]]).
4. [[Dan. 4:3]], [[Dan. 4:34]]: restored, Nebuchadnezzar praises the God “whose dominion is an everlasting dominion” (see also the note on [[Dan. 4:1]]).
5. [[Dan. 5:26–28]]: Belshazzar hears “God hath numbered thy kingdom, and finished it” (see the note on [[Dan. 5:25]]).
6. [[Dan. 6:26]]: Darius decrees of Daniel's God, “his kingdom that which shall not be destroyed.”
7. [[Dan. 7:12–14]]: the beasts “had their dominion taken away,” and the Son of man is given “an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away” (see the note on [[Dan. 7:13]]).
8. [[Dan. 7:18]], [[Dan. 7:27]]: “the saints of the most High shall take the kingdom.”
9. [[D&C 65:2]]: the stone rolls forth “until it has filled the whole earth.”
