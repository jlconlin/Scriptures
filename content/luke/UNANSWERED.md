# Luke: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** How Mary, of David's line, was a relative of Elisabeth, of Aaron's (1:36). The writer listed this as unanswered, but the checker found that Brown's ch. 1 ¶177 addresses it (the word means only 'relative', and Mary may have priestly ancestry). I added no note for it.
- **5:** Why Luke says 'lake of Gennesaret' rather than 'sea of Galilee' (from the writer's report; not researched); What 'murmured' (5:30) recalls (left open in the story file).
- **6:** Why Luke puts the cloak before the coat (6:29); Who the false prophets of 6:26 are.
- **8:** Why Luke alone leaves 'sister' out of the family saying (8:21): the writer found Brown names it only as a puzzle.
- **18:** Luke 18:30: what is the 'manifold more in this present time'? The writer left it to Mark 10's note on the hundredfold.
- **3:** Who the soldiers of 3:14 were: only Ellicott speaks to it (possibly Antipas's troops), and his Aretas war is dated after the ministry. No Church or BYU source was found.
- **8:** 8:51: why Jesus chose these three, Peter, James and John. Talmage names the occasions but gives no reason.
