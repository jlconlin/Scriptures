# 2 Chronicles: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Why does Chronicles keep God's appearing at Gibeon (1:7) but drop the word "dream" that Kings uses? No source read gives a reason.
- **4:** Whether the Hebrew 'abiw at 2 Chr. 4:16 (and 'abi at 2:13) is part of the craftsman's name (Huram-abi) rather than a title: no Church or Latter-day Saint source read in this chapter addresses it.
- **10:** Why does a book written after the exile still say "unto this day" (10:19)? No source read explains it.
- **11:** 2 Chr. 11:5–12: why Rehoboam fortified these fifteen towns, and against whom. No Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source answers. Keil reads them as a defense against Egypt built after Shishak's invasion; Cambridge reads them as a way to keep Judah under control.
- **12:** What "concerning genealogies" means in the title of Iddo's book (12:15); Why the text names Rehoboam's mother, Naamah an Ammonitess (12:13); Why Rehoboam forsook the law just when his kingdom became strong (12:1).
