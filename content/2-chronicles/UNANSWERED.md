# 2 Chronicles: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Why does Chronicles keep God's appearing at Gibeon (1:7) but drop the word "dream" that Kings uses? No source read gives a reason.
- **4:** Whether the Hebrew 'abiw at 2 Chr. 4:16 (and 'abi at 2:13) is part of the craftsman's name (Huram-abi) rather than a title: no Church or Latter-day Saint source read in this chapter addresses it.
- **10:** Why does a book written after the exile still say "unto this day" (10:19)? No source read explains it.
- **11:** 2 Chr. 11:5–12: why Rehoboam fortified these fifteen towns, and against whom. No Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source answers. Keil reads them as a defense against Egypt built after Shishak's invasion; Cambridge reads them as a way to keep Judah under control.
- **12:** What "concerning genealogies" means in the title of Iddo's book (12:15); Why the text names Rehoboam's mother, Naamah an Ammonitess (12:13); Why Rehoboam forsook the law just when his kingdom became strong (12:1).
- **14:** Who Zerah the Cushite was: no Latter-day Saint source read identifies him (14:9); How Chronicles' ten quiet years (14:1) and its 'no more war unto the five and thirtieth year' (15:19) fit with Kings' 'war between Asa and Baasha … all their days': no Latter-day Saint source read takes it up.
- **15:** Why are Simeonites, whose land lay in the south, among the northerners who came to Asa (15:9)? Only older commentators' guesses; no Latter-day Saint source; Why does 15:8 call the message 'the prophecy of Oded the prophet' when verse 1 names Azariah? Only older commentaries (text damage or a gloss); the note was cut under the brief's limit; What were the things Abijah dedicated (15:18)? Only an older commentary's guess.
- **17:** 2 Chr. 17:9: no Church or Latter-day Saint source read identifies which book 'the book of the law of the LORD' was. Only Keil calls it the Pentateuch, and the chapter now leaves Keil out.
- **19:** No Latter-day Saint source explains what the 'wrath' of 19:2 was, or who 'returned to Jerusalem' in 19:8; only the older commentaries do.
- **21:** Why Jehoram killed his brothers (21:4). Only Keil offers a motive; no Latter-day Saint source answers it, and the note now says the text gives none; How Elijah's writing reached Jehoram after Elijah's translation (21:12). No Latter-day Saint source read takes up the timing.
- **25:** What was a hundred talents of silver worth to Judah (25:6, 9)? No source read for this chapter answers it; Why would Amaziah worship the gods of the Edomites he had just defeated (25:14)? No Latter-day Saint source answers it; only Keil and the Cambridge Bible do.
- **26:** Why Uzziah wanted to burn incense, beyond the pride the text names in 26:16. Now that Keil's high-priest reading is cut, no Latter-day Saint source answers this; What Zechariah of 26:5 was: prophet, priest or teacher. No Church source says.
- **28:** Why does 28:19 call Ahaz 'king of Israel'? Only Keil addresses it; no Church or BYU source does (writer's question 7; checker's finding 13); Does 2 Chr. 28:15 (Jericho, the naked clothed, anointing, the weak carried on donkeys) lie behind the parable of the Good Samaritan in Luke 10? A search of Church, BYU, Scripture Central and Interpreter sources found none that makes the connection.
- **34:** Why does Chronicles date Josiah's reform ten years before the book was found, when Kings ties the reform to the book? Two older commentaries disagree, and no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source takes the question up; Why were the Levite musicians put over the masons (34:12)? Only one older commentary offers a reason.
- **35:** 35:22: no source read explains how Huldah's promise that Josiah would be buried 'in peace' (34:28) fits his death in battle beyond what the 34:28 and 2 Kgs. 22:20 notes say.
