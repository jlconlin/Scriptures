# Numbers: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

## Numbers 13

- 13:22: why the text dates Hebron by Zoan in Egypt.
- 13:20: the season of the first-ripe grapes (only Keil gives it).

## Numbers 19

- 19:3: why Eleazar and not Aaron oversees the rite, and why it is done outside the camp (only Keil answers).
- 19:12: why the third and the seventh days, and why seven days of uncleanness (only Keil answers).

## Numbers 21

- 21:7: whether the LORD took the serpents away, as the people asked.
