# Numbers: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

## Numbers 1

- 1:2 why this census was commanded when David's (2 Sam. 24) was a sin, beyond the readings at Ex. 30:12.

## Numbers 2

- 2:3–31 why the tribes are grouped by their mothers (Leah, Rachel, the handmaids).
- 2:10 why Reuben, the firstborn, leads only the second camp.

## Numbers 3

- 3:12 whether the Levites' own firstborn were counted or exchanged (3:40–45).
- 3:41 why the Levites' cattle were not matched head for head, as the sons were.

## Numbers 4

- 4:6 why only the ark has its colored cloth outside (only an older commentary).
- 4:3 with 8:24, why two starting ages (only older readings).

## Numbers 5

- 5:17 why dust from the tabernacle floor and why a clay vessel (only Keil's symbolism).
- 5:22 what the woman's "Amen, amen" does.

## Numbers 6

- 6:2 why a woman could take the vow, and how often women did.

## Numbers 7

- 7:3 what the "covered wagons" were (the word is uncertain).
- 7:12–83 why twelve identical gifts, in a Latter-day Saint reading (no source).

## Numbers 9

- 9:6 who the men defiled by a dead body were, and whose body.
- 9:5–14 whether Israel kept any Passover between Sinai and Gilgal (Josh. 5:10).

## Numbers 10

- 10:21 whether the ark on the march was among "the sanctuary" the Kohathites carried or went apart from it (10:33); only Keil harmonizes.
- 10:2 a Latter-day Saint reading of the silver trumpets (none found).

## Numbers 11

- 11:1 what the people complained about at Taberah, and what the fire was.
- 11:26 why Eldad and Medad stayed in the camp.
- 11:31 what "two cubits high" means in practice.

## Numbers 12

- 12:16 where Hazeroth was (no Bible Dictionary entry).

## Numbers 13

- 13:22: why the text dates Hebron by Zoan in Egypt.
- 13:20: the season of the first-ripe grapes (only Keil gives it).

## Numbers 14

- 14:37 what the plague was that killed the ten spies.
- 14:22 which ten events "ten times" refers to (no Latter-day Saint source settles it).

## Numbers 15

- 15:32 why gathering sticks in particular (for a fire on the Sabbath, Ex.
- 35:3).
- 15:38 why blue (the Student Manual's answer comes from Fallows, not read).

## Numbers 16

- 16:41 why the congregation, the day after, blamed Moses and Aaron for killing "the people of the LORD".
- 16:46 why incense, which had just brought death on the 250, stopped the plague in Aaron's hand.

## Numbers 17

- 17:8 whether the almond rod is meant to recall the almond-blossom cups of the candlestick (Ex.
- 25:33).
- 17:8 a Latter-day Saint reading of the budding rod as a type of Christ or the Resurrection (none found).

## Numbers 18

- 18:7 what "within the veil" means here (Keil and Cambridge differ).

## Numbers 19

- 19:3: why Eleazar and not Aaron oversees the rite, and why it is done outside the camp (only Keil answers).
- 19:12: why the third and the seventh days, and why seven days of uncleanness (only Keil answers).

## Numbers 20

- 20:24 what "gathered unto his people" means.
- 20:1 why Miriam's death is told so briefly.
- 20:16 which angel "brought us forth out of Egypt".

## Numbers 21

- 21:7: whether the LORD took the serpents away, as the people asked.

## Numbers 22

- 22:39 where Kirjath-huzoth was, and what Balak's sacrifices in verse 40 were for.
- 22:22 who the angel of the LORD is in this chapter.

## Numbers 23

- 23:9 what "the people shall dwell alone, and shall not be reckoned among the nations" means (only Keil read).
- 23:4 why Balaam tells God about his altars.
- 23:10 what reading lies behind the modern translation's "dust cloud of Israel".

## Numbers 24

- 24:6–7 what "water out of his buckets," the "lign aloes" and the cedars "beside the waters" mean (only Keil and Cambridge, who disagree).
- 24:21 a possible wordplay between "Kenite" and "nest".

## Numbers 25

- 25:14 why the text names Zimri and Cozbi.
- 25:9 why Paul has 23,000 (1 Cor.
- 10:8) where Numbers has 24,000 (only Keil).
- 25:4 whether the hanging of 25:4 or Moses' order in 25:5 was carried out.

## Numbers 26

- 26:46 why Asher's daughter Sarah is named.
- 26:14 why Simeon fell from 59,300 to 22,200 (no source links it to Zimri at Peor, 25:14).
- 26:54 how "thou shalt give" fits Moses not entering Canaan.

## Numbers 27

- 27:8 why the law of 27:8–11 gives a widow no place.
