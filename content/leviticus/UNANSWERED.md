# Leviticus: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** Why is the flock animal killed on the north side of the altar (1:11)?; Why is the bird's crop thrown east of the altar, by the place of the ashes (1:16)?; Why is the bird torn open by the wings but not divided in two (1:17)?.
- **2:** Why all the frankincense goes on the altar while only a handful of the flour does (2:2, 16): no source read explains it.
- **3:** Why female animals were allowed in the peace offering but not in the herd or flock burnt offering (no source read gives a reason); Lev. 3:11, does the LORD 'eat'? The only answer found is Keil's (the food reached the LORD by being burned); no Latter-day Saint source addresses the question directly.
- **7:** Why the third day, not some other limit, for eating the peace offering (7:17–18): no Church, BYU, Scripture Central or Interpreter source found; only Keil's older reading about spoilage; How the waving of the wave offering was done (7:30): the text does not say, and only Keil (also printed in the Student Manual) describes it.
- **9:** 9:17: what was 'the burnt sacrifice of the morning'? It is presumably the daily offering of Ex. 29:38–42, but no note says so. This is a gap in depth, not something no source could answer; 9:23: why Moses and Aaron went into the tent before the glory appeared. Only one study's tentative reading speaks to it.
- **10:** 10:1: No source the chapter could use says what the "strange fire" was.
- **12:** Why the periods are doubled for a daughter (12:5). No source knows; the Student Manual says the question has 'no key at present'.
- **13:** What the 'scall' and the 'thin yellow hair' of 13:30–37 were medically: no Latter-day Saint source that was read says.
- **14:** Lev. 14:10: what the eighth day signifies. The brief makes 14:10 the place where the eighth day is explained, but no source read (Keil, Cambridge, Welch) gives it a meaning; Lev. 14:34: why the LORD says 'I put the plague of leprosy in a house'. Only Keil answers it, and that reading was cut to stay within the limit on Bible Hub commentaries; Lev. 14:8: why the man stays seven days outside his tent. The text gives no reason; the writer left out Keil's Targum reading.
- **16:** Why were lots cast over the two goats (16:8)?; Why is the Day of Atonement in the autumn? KnoWhy 849's answer rests on the Mishnah, so it was not used; Who was the 'fit man' (16:21)? No source goes beyond the footnote's 'appointed'.
- **17:** Why the laws of 17:8–16 bind the stranger as well as Israel: no source read gives a reason; Why the blood of game is covered with dust in particular, beyond the text's own reason that the blood is the life: no Latter-day Saint source read says.
- **19:** 19:36: what the hin was as a measure. The chapter names it, but no source in the chapter defines it, so the note leaves it out.
- **21:** 21:9: why a priest's daughter is burned with fire rather than stoned; no source read explains the penalty.
- **22:** 22:23: why an animal with a limb too long or too short could be a freewill offering but not pay a vow. The text gives no reason, and no source read here explains it.
- **24:** 24:14: why the witnesses laid their hands on the blasphemer's head. Only Keil (an older reading) answers it; no Latter-day Saint source read does; 24:2–4: no source read says what the lamp and the bread signified beyond Exodus; the chapter points to Ex. 25:30 and 27:20.
- **25:** 25:35–36: whether the ban on interest covered the 'stranger, or a sojourner' whom verse 35 says to help (the writer's report, item 13); 25:29–31: why a house in a walled city could be sold for good, beyond verse 31's 'counted as the fields of the country'.
