# Nehemiah: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** When the wall of 1:3 was last broken down. No Latter-day Saint source says, and the text does not date it.
- **2:** 2:12–16: why Nehemiah surveyed the walls by night and in secret. Only Ludlow answers it (to keep it from Sanballat), and the brief's one-point limit on Ludlow excluded him.
- **5:** Neh. 5:11: whether "the hundredth part" was a monthly one percent interest charge. Only Keil says so (probably), and the plain text keeps it as interest on his row.
- **6:** How the wall could be finished in fifty-two days: no Latter-day Saint source explains it; only Keil does, and the note labels that as one older explanation.
- **7:** 7:4: why Jerusalem was still so thinly settled almost a century after the first return. No source read answers it.
- **10:** Why the people pledged a third of a shekel when the law asked a half (10:32): no Church or Latter-day Saint source read answers it. Keil's guess is the people's poverty.
- **13:** What became of the wives and children of 13:23–28 (the brief already lists this one); Whether the Eliashib of 13:4 is the high priest Eliashib of 3:1 and 13:28: no source read settles it.
