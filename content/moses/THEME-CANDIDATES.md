## Reported by chapters 1–8 (not yet sorted)

- (1) The Only Begotten named throughout the book (1:6, 13, 16, 17, 19, 21, 32, 33).
- (1) Satan imitating Christ and being dismissed in the Son's name (1:12-22; compare Moses 4 and 5).
- (1) Creation by the Word, the Only Begotten (1:32-33; carried into 2:1).
- (1) The book as restored 'plain and most precious' words (1:23, 41).
- (1) God's work as bringing His children into His presence: Moses on the mountain, Israel at Sinai, Zion (1:1-11, 39).
- (1) Who is speaking: the Son speaking in the Father's name (1:6; 'I, God' from 2:1).
- (2) The Only Begotten named as Creator and as the one addressed (2:1, 26-27; with Moses 1:32-33).
- (2) 'I, God': the first-person voice (2:3-31; Moses 3:2, 3, 9, 19).
- (2) The Creation read through the work and glory of Moses 1:39 (2:1).
- (2) Light and darkness, from Moses' encounter with Satan to the first day (Moses 1:12-26; 2:2-5).
- (2) Who is speaking: the Father's words and the Son's role (Moses 1:2-8; 2:1, 26).
- (3) The Only Begotten named in the Creation account (Moses 1:32-33; 2:1, 26; 3:18).
- (3) Spiritual before natural creation (3:5, 7, 9; D&C 29:32).
- (3) Agency given in Eden alongside commandment (3:17; 7:32; D&C 29:35).
- (3) Adam as a premortal spirit who took a body, "like Christ" (3:5, 7; 1:34; the 1909 First Presidency statement).
- (4) The Only Begotten named throughout the book (4:1, 3, 28; in 4:28 the Father speaks to Him where Genesis has only 'us').
- (4) Satan's dealings with Moses, Eve and Cain: he poses as the Only Begotten (1:19), then offers 'I will be thy son' (4:1) and speaks through the serpent (4:6-7).
- (4) The council in heaven across Restoration scripture (Moses 4:1-4; Abr. 3:22-28; D&C 29:36-39; D&C 76:25-27).
- (4) God asks questions of those who have fallen (4:15-19; 5:34-35).
- (5) The gospel taught from the beginning by voice, angels and the Holy Ghost: 5:4-9, 5:12, 5:58-59.
- (5) Sacrifice as a similitude of the Only Begotten: 5:5-8, 5:20.
- (5) Satan's dealings with the first family and with Cain (a claim, a command, then an oath): 5:13, 5:18, 5:23, 5:29-31.
- (5) Secret combinations from Cain to the Book of Mormon: 5:29-31, 5:49-51.
- (5) Shut out from the Lord's presence, and the way back: 5:4, 5:41.
- (5) The Only Begotten named and the name of the Son: 5:7-9, 5:57.
- (6) The gospel taught from the beginning: repentance, baptism, and the Holy Ghost given to Adam in the name of the Only Begotten (6:52-68; continued in 7:1).
- (6) Records and the book of remembrance: 6:5-8 and 6:46, with Malachi 3:16 and D&C 85:9 and 128:24.
- (6) Becoming sons of God: 6:68 and 7:1, with D&C 34:3 and 35:2 from the same weeks of 1830.
- (6) Names of the Father and the Son: Man of Holiness and Son of Man (6:57; 7:35).
- (6) Satan's dominion and secret oaths: 6:15 and 6:28-29, following Cain in 5:29-31.
- (6) The prophet's call and his protest: Enoch 'slow of speech' (6:31-32) beside Moses in Exodus 4:10.
- (7) Zion: a people, a city, taken up and returning (7:18–21, 62–64, 69; D&C 97:21; JST Gen. 9:21).
- (7) God weeps, the heavens weep, Enoch weeps, the earth mourns (7:28–41, 44, 48–49).
- (7) The Only Begotten named and known from the beginning: Chosen, the Righteous, Lamb slain, Messiah, King of Zion, Rock of Heaven (7:39, 45–47, 50, 53).
- (7) The covenant with Enoch carried through Noah (7:50–52, 60; JST Gen. 9; Moses 8).
- (7) The spirits in prison and the salvation of the dead (7:38–39, 57).
- (7) Satan's dominion: the chain, the laughter, 'Satan shall be their father' (7:24–26, 37).
- (8) The gospel taught from the beginning, in the name of Jesus Christ: Moses 8:16, 19, 24, with 6:52 and 6:64-65.
- (8) The Lord's weeping and Noah's grief over the same people: Moses 8:25-26, with 7:28-40.
- (8) The spirits in prison and the gospel preached to the dead: Moses 8:30, with 7:38-39 and D&C 138.
- (8) The priesthood after the order of the Son of God handed down from Adam to Noah: Moses 8:19, with 6:67-68 and D&C 107.
- (8) Creation undone and a new creation: Moses 8:22, 26, with 2:31 and 1:39.
- (8) The curse on the ground from Adam to Cain to the famine: Moses 8:4, 9, with 4:23 and 5:37.
