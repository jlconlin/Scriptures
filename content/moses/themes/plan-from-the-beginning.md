In Moses the gospel is not a late arrival. What changes is who carries it, not what is taught: an angel and God’s own voice teach Adam, Adam and Eve teach their children, Enoch repeats to his generation what God said to Adam, and Noah preaches “even as our fathers.”

## God’s work and the Son
Before the book comes to Adam it states God’s purpose and names the Son: “This is my work and my glory—to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man” ([[Moses 1:39]]), and “by mine Only Begotten I created these things” ([[Moses 2:1]]).

## The angel and the sacrifice
Adam and Eve are commanded to “offer the firstlings of their flocks” ([[Moses 5:5]]). An angel asks why, and Adam answers, “I know not, save the Lord commanded me” ([[Moses 5:6]]). The angel explains: “This thing is a similitude of the sacrifice of the Only Begotten of the Father, which is full of grace and truth. Wherefore, thou shalt do all that thou doest in the name of the Son, and thou shalt repent and call upon God in the name of the Son forevermore” ([[Moses 5:7–8]]). See the notes in [Moses 5](/moses/5/).

## Adam and Eve understand, and teach
The Holy Ghost then speaks the Son’s words: “as thou hast fallen thou mayest be redeemed, and all mankind, even as many as will” ([[Moses 5:9]]). Adam answers, “in the flesh I shall see God” ([[Moses 5:10]]). Eve: “Were it not for our transgression we never should have had seed, and never should have known good and evil, and the joy of our redemption” ([[Moses 5:11]]). Then “they made all things known unto their sons and their daughters” ([[Moses 5:12]]).

The chapter says it outright: “thus the Gospel began to be preached, from the beginning” ([[Moses 5:58]]; see the note there). “Gospel” occurs in Moses only at 5:58, 5:59 and 8:19.[@lds-scriptures]

## “This is the plan of salvation”
In his sermon Enoch reports God’s words to Adam. God “called upon our father Adam by his own voice” ([[Moses 6:51]]) and told him to be “baptized, even in water, in the name of mine Only Begotten Son, who is full of grace and truth, which is Jesus Christ” ([[Moses 6:52]]). Then: “This is the plan of salvation unto all men, through the blood of mine Only Begotten, who shall come in the meridian of time” ([[Moses 6:62]]). Adam is baptized ([[Moses 6:64–66]]).

In the text, “plan of salvation” occurs only at 6:62, and “Jesus Christ” first at 6:52, in words spoken to Adam.[@lds-scriptures] See [Moses 6](/moses/6/).

## Enoch’s charge and vision
Enoch is commanded to baptize ([[Moses 7:11]]). He sees the day of the Son of Man and says, “the Lamb is slain from the foundation of the world” ([[Moses 7:47]]); he sees “the Son of Man lifted up on the cross” ([[Moses 7:55]]). See [Moses 7](/moses/7/) and [The Father’s One Plan and Satan’s Rebellion](/moses/themes/one-plan-and-rebellion/).

## Noah
Noah “taught the things of God, even as it was in the beginning” ([[Moses 8:16]]) and is ordained to “declare his Gospel unto the children of men, even as it was given unto Enoch” ([[Moses 8:19]]). He preaches: “Believe and repent of your sins and be baptized in the name of Jesus Christ, the Son of God, even as our fathers, and ye shall receive the Holy Ghost” ([[Moses 8:24]]). See [Moses 8](/moses/8/).

## The same words
Set side by side: “in the name of the Son” ([[Moses 5:8]]), “in the name of mine Only Begotten Son” ([[Moses 6:52]]), “in the name of the Father, and of the Son” ([[Moses 7:11]]) and “in the name of Jesus Christ, the Son of God” ([[Moses 8:24]]). (The phrase is also at 1:17, 1:21, 4:1, 7:50 and 7:59.) “Full of grace and truth” is said of the Only Begotten at 1:6 and 1:32, and then at 5:7, 6:52 and 7:11.[@lds-scriptures] The Holy Ghost “beareth record of the Father and the Son” at 1:24, 5:9 and 7:11, the three places the words occur in the book. Enoch says “our father Adam taught these things” ([[Moses 7:1]]); Noah says “even as our fathers.” The comparison is this page’s own.

## From the beginning
After the Fall “God conversed with men, and made known unto them the plan of redemption, which had been prepared from the foundation of the world” ([[Alma 12:30]]).[@lds-scriptures]

The plan of salvation “has been revealed and taught in every dispensation beginning with Adam and is the same in every age of the world.”[@bd-dispensations] An Apostle called Adam and Eve “this planet’s first humans and first Christians.”[@maxwell-from-the-beginning-1993] A letter of March 1834, sent in the name of the elders of the Church in Kirtland[@jsp-letter-1834-03] and printed by the Church among Joseph Smith’s teachings,[@tpjs-2007-ch-3] says that “all that were ever saved, were saved through the power of this great plan of redemption, as much so before the coming of Christ as since.”[@jsp-letter-1834-03]

Adam’s own chapter ends on it: the Only Begotten Son “should come in the meridian of time, who was prepared from before the foundation of the world” ([[Moses 5:57]]).
