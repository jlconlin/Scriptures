# John: reader questions no source answered

Questions a reader would ask that no source read answered, by chapter: a list for a later deepening pass. Kept out of `BRIEF.md` so the brief stays brief.

- **1:** 1:9: is 'the true Light, which lighteth every man' the Light of Christ? The writer cut this for selectivity; 1:17 / JST 1:17–18: why the law came by Moses and grace and truth by Jesus Christ. The writer found no source that discusses the JST wording.
- **2:** Was it really wine, given the Word of Wisdom (2:3–10)? The writer left it out; the original 1836 journal was not opened; Who are 'his brethren' (2:12)? Not answered in this chapter.
- **4:** 4:34: what 'My meat is to do the will of him that sent me, and to finish his work' means beyond the plain words (from the writer's report); 4:6: why John notes that Jesus was wearied (from the writer's report).
- **5:** 5:5: Does 'thirty and eight years' signify anything? Only one study's typology (Deut. 2:14) was found, and the writer cut it; 5:37: 'Ye have neither heard his voice … nor seen his shape.' No Latter-day Saint source read takes the verse up beside the prophets who have seen God; 5:43: Who is 'another' who 'shall come in his own name'? Nothing found beyond speculation about false messiahs; 5:31 beside 8:14: how 'my witness is not true' agrees with 'my record is true' is not taken up from a source.
- **7:** 7:24: what 'your traditions' in the JST refers to. Only a manual links it to D&C 93:39; 7:10: why Jesus went up in secret, and by which road.
- **8:** 8:25 'Even the same that I said unto you from the beginning': the Greek is obscure, and no source the writer read explains it (from the writer's report).
- **14:** 14:3: what 'I will come again, and receive you' refers to (the Second Coming, death, or something else). No Latter-day Saint source the writer read settles it; 14:26: whether 'bring all things to your remembrance' bears on the apostles' written witness. No source read says so.
- **19:** Why does the Joseph Smith Translation read 'burial' for 'skull' at 19:17? No source read explains the change; What does the garden and the new tomb of 19:41 signify? No source read gives it a meaning beyond the fact.
- **20:** 20:9: which scripture foretold that He must rise. No source read names one; 20:1: why the Joseph Smith Translation reads 'two angels sitting thereon.' No source read explains it.
