## Reported by chapters 1–5 (not yet sorted)

- (1) 'None to comfort her': the comforter refrain (1:2, 9, 16, 17, 21; the same root as a verb in 2:13).
- (1) The Lord as the one who afflicts, with the cause stated (1:5, 12-15, 18; carried on in 2:1-8).
- (1) The city as a woman: widow, daughter of Zion, virgin daughter of Judah, unclean woman (1:1, 6, 8-9, 15, 17).
- (1) 'Behold / see, O LORD': the appeals that the Lord look (1:9, 11, 20).
- (1) Prayers against enemies left to the Lord's justice (1:21-22; 3:64-66; 4:21-22).
- (2) The Lord as the one who strikes, 'as an enemy' (2:1-8, 2:17; also 1:12-15, 3:1-18), with the book's own cause (1:18) and 3:33.
- (2) No answer from the Lord; no law and no vision (2:9; the prayers of 2:20-22 left unanswered).
- (2) moed, the appointed feast or meeting, turned against the city (1:4, 1:15, 2:6, 2:7, 2:22).
- (2) 'Behold and consider': prayers that ask the Lord only to look (2:20; also 1:11 and 5:1, not checked in this chapter).
- (2) City lament as a form, shared with Mormon 6:16-22 (2:11).
- (3) The Lord seeing: "see", "look", "behold" (1:9, 1:11, 2:20, 3:1, 3:50, 3:59-60, 5:1).
- (3) Prayer shut out and prayer heard (3:8, 3:44, 3:55-57).
- (3) The unnamed "he" who afflicts; the Lord as an enemy (2:1-8; 3:1-17).
- (3) Remembering (3:19-21; 5:1).
- (3) The book's own account of the cause, and of the Lord's heart (1:5, 1:18; 3:31-33, 3:39-42; 5:16).
- (4) The covenant curses of Deuteronomy 28 as the frame of the siege (4:10, 4:16, 4:19).
- (4) The cup of the Lord's fury passing to Edom (4:21; Jeremiah 25).
- (4) The king as "the anointed of the LORD", the people's breath and shadow (4:20).
- (4) Daughter of Zion and daughter of Edom (4:21-22).
- (4) Shifts of voice: "they", "we", "thou" (4:13-22).
- (5) "Turn" (shuv): 5:21, with Jer. 31:18 and the Lord's reply in Jer. 31:20.
- (5) "Remember" and "forget": 5:1 and 5:20.
- (5) The fathers' sin and the people's own: 5:7 and 5:16, with Jer. 31:29-30 and Ezek. 18.
- (5) The Lord does not answer: 5:19-22, and the ending of the book.
